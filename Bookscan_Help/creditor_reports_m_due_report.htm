Creditor Report:         Due Report


   Purpose:
    View all credit due to a nominated date.

   Parameters:
    Due date, Currencies to be included.

   Output:
    Key Dates, Invoice-number, Amount and home-currency amount (if applic).

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle      -   Reflects selection criteria.
    HomeCurrency      -   The code for the local currency.
    ShowHome      -   Controls whether the report prints currency conversions.

See also:   Selection Controls.
