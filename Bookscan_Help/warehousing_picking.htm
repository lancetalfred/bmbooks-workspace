Warehousing - Picking Slip Control
Previous  Top  Next


The Picking Slip Control window is used by the store persons to print out picking slips. It is also used my managers to monitor the warehouse work that is outstanding.

The window is opened from the main menu: Modules/ Warehousing/ Picking.
The window header will display:
·a summary of all unprinted picking slips and  
·a summary of picking slips that are being picked but have not yet been packed and invoiced.  
The browser will default to listing all unprinted picking slips.

Printing picking slips
Select the picking slips to be printed and press the Print button (F12).
You can select picking slips in several ways:
·A single line - click on it  
·Multiple lines - hold the Control key down and click on the lines required.  
·A group of lines - click on the first line while holding down the Shift key. Click on the last line of the group. All intervening lines will be selected.  
·First five lines - click on the Next 5 Lines button (F5). Clicking on the button again will add the next five to the selection.  
·Clearing selections - click on the Clear Selections button (F6) or just click on another line in the browser.  

Held picking slips
Picking slips below the minimum order level are 'held" so that they can't be printed. Held picking slips display a tick in the Held column of the browser. You can manually set a picking slip as held by double clicking on it.

Reprinting
Click on the Show Printed button. The browser will display all printed but uninvoiced picking slips. Select the picking slips and click the Print button (F12). Click on the Finish Reprinting button when finished.

Forward orders
Forward order picking slips are not displayed in the browser until the forward order date. Then they are treated as standard picking slips.
To display forward orders that are not yet due, click on the Forward Orders button (F8). The summary fields in the header will now show the totals only for forward orders. The browser will display only outstanding forward orders. The picking slips can be printed in the usual way. Click the Hide Fwd Orders button when finished.

Stopped Accounts
Picking slips for customers whose accounts are on stop are displayed in red in the browser. These picking slips will not print until the customer is taken off stop.
