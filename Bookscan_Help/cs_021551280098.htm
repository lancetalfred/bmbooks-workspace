Printing Special Orders Reports
Previous  Top  Next


From the main menu, click on Sales, then point to Specials and then click on Print.

Customer reports

These produce Special Order Status Reports for customers.
These are letters addressed to customers listing the outstanding titles on their special orders.

All
The letter lists all special orders for a selected customer.
             
Single
Select a single special order.
Click on the in the special order box and select the order number required from the list displayed.
      
Category   
Report to all customers with outstanding special orders in a selected category.

Other Reports

Status    
Creates a report in customer order, reporting outstanding special orders for titles with a selected print status (eg NYP).
   
All   
Produces a summary of all special orders.
Parameters:
· Order numbers greater than.  
· Report unsupplied items only option.  
· Sort by either customer or by special order number.  

Not Found
Lists all special orders where the ISBN is unknown.

Overdue   
Reports on overdue special orders.
Parameters:
· Start and finish dates.  
Click here for assistance with changing dates.  
· Standing orders.  
Select whether to include or exclude standing orders or whether to report on them exclusively.           
· Supplier.  
To select multiple suppliers, hold the control key down while clicking on the required suppliers.  
Click the Select All button to report on all suppliers.
