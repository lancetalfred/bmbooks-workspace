Valuation Report:      Department (single)


   Purpose:
Value all stock on-hand for a nominated department with the optional inclusion of all departments which are consolidated with it. Department codes are consolidated in sharing their first character.  

   Parameters:
    Department, option to consolidate.

   Output:
    Title & Item counts, Stock value at: Last & Average costs, Sell & R.r.p; Approval values, Average cost.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle       -   reflects selection.  


See also:   Selection Controls.
