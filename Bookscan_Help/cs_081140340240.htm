e-Comms: Configuring Suppliers
Previous  Top 


On the main menu of Bookscan click on Tables, then click Supplier Master.
Select the supplier you are setting up and open the Address tab.
Click on the Edit button to enable 'edit mode'.

EDI - Pacstream
Enter PACSTREAM in the EDI field.
In the SAN field enter in the number given to you by your supplier.



Then click Save button once the changes have been made.

EDI - Email
Enter EMAIL in the EDI field.
In the SAN field enter in the number given to you by your supplier, (if any).



Then click Save button once the changes have been made.
