The Sales Rep Master is the section of Bookscan where you can store and manage the Sales Reps that your business has.
To open the Sales Rep Master, on the main menu click on Tables, then move the mouse arrow onto Authority Tables so its sub menu shows, then click on Sales Reps.



As shown above when the Sales Rep Master is loaded it look this. In the top half are the fields to be entered for entering a new Rep and at the bottom the Reps within the database are listed.
To leave the Sales Rep Master at any time click Exit button.

Adding a sales rep:
To add a new sales rep click on the Add button.
This will clear the text box fields, which indicates that you need to type in the details.
The minimum fields required as Rep Code (maximum 4 characters) and Name (maximum 30 characters).
The other fields are optional.
Next click Save button. This will save the new Rep
Otherwise click Clear to not save the record.


Editing a sales rep:
To make changes to an existing sales rep, simply click on the rep from the listing in the bottom half of the window.
Click on the Edit button.
This will display the reps details in the top half of the window in the same fields as when the rep was entered.
Type in your changes.
Note: the Rep Code cannot be changed.
Click the Save button. This will save the changes.
Otherwise click Undo button, which will leave the sales rep unchanged.
