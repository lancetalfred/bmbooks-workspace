Minimum stock levels
The default minimum stock level is set on the Titles tab of System Options. When adding new products to the title master, the minimum order field will display this default level. The default level will also be set when importing products using the Import Title window and when importing direct from a web site.

Draft Purchase Orders window
The Draft Purchase Orders window can be opened from the main menu: Purchasing/Draft Order/Add/Change.
When you open the window, the drafts will be filtered by the first supplier, sorted alphabetically, to have orders in the draft. To view different suppliers, select them from the Supplier combo box.
Shortcut
If you have the browser filtered by supplier, you can navigate quickly to other suppliers that have draft orders, using the arrow buttons on either side of the supplier combo box.



Unfiltered view
To remove the supplier filter, untick the Supplier Filter (F6) checkbox; the browser will display all draft titles. Now as you click different lines in the browser, the Supplier combo box will display the supplier of the currently highlighted draft order.
To reset the filter either:
·tick the Supplier Filter (F6) checkbox. The browser will filter to the supplier in the Supplier combo box,  
·select a new supplier in the Supplier combo box.  

Supplier details
The currently selected supplier's details are displayed in the left, top of the screen.
The supplier's setting for EDI ordering is displayed in ared box next the supplier's name. The options are:
·Pacstream – uses the Pacstream service to transmit orders,  
·Email – orders will be emailed to the supplier,  
·No EDI – you will have to print and fax or post the order.  

Browser
The browser displays either all the draft orders or just those for the currently selected supplier.
The browser lines are displayed in alphabetical order either by title or by author, depending on which option has been selected in the Purchasing tab of System Options.
Shortcut
If you right click on the browser and select Skip to Author/Title, a window will open where you can enter the title or author you require. Just enter the first few letters of your search e.g. 'Harry' or 'Rowl', and then click the OK button. The browser cursor will then move to the selected title/author.

Browser options
Put a title on hold
Putting a title on hold marks it as not to be included on the purchase order. You can do this by double clicking in the Hold column on the browser.

Delete a draft order line.
Highlight the lines in the browser you wish to delete, open the context menu (right click on the window) and select Delete from the drop down menu.

Filter the draft orders by origin
Products can appear on the draft from a variety of Origins.
To filter titles by their source, click on the Refined Filter (F9) button or open the context menu then select 'Filter by source'. Click on the filter you require and click OK.



Source codes include:
·Sp   Special  
·Bi   Bisac files  
·Bo   Routed from purchase order  
·Re   Regenerated  
·In   Invoice backorders  
·Te   Academic module  
·Op   Operator  
·PO   Entered via the Purchase Order entry window.  
Other non source code filter items are:
·Non-specials only  
·No academic titles  
·Filter by imprint  
·Only titles on hold  
·Only unheld titles  
·Lift filter  

Change Supplier
To change the supplier you wish to order from, highlight the Lines in the browser you wish to move to a new supplier. Click on the Swap Supplier (F7) button. A pop up window will open where you can select the new supplier. Click one of the save buttons or press the Enter key to complete the move.

Department ordering
You can also prepare your purchase orders by department although you will need to switch back to supplier mode to finalise them.
·Right click on the window to open the context menu and select Department ordering.  
·The supplier drop-down box will change to listing all the departments, with the first department automatically selected.  
·Select the department you require; the browser will display on lines for products from that department.  
·Process each line just the same as you would for ordering by supplier. All the filtering options also work the same way.  

Draft Purchase Orders: Edit window
You can examine each draft order line in detail. Highlight a line in the browser and click on the Edit button (or just double click on the line) to go into detailed view.
For a more detailed view, click on a title and then the Edit button (or double click on the title) to go into detail view.



Here you can amend the following fields:
·Change the order quantity.  
·Add purchase order comments which will appear on the purchase order against that line.  
·Change the minimum and maximum stock levels.  
·Place the draft order on Hold.  
·Mark the draft order as a consignment order (consignment module only)  
·Change the title's status.  
·Change the titles master comment.  
·Change the title to a firm.  

Browsers
Receiving History browser
The receiving history window displays each time the title was received into stock, transferred to another store, returned to supplier or made into a kit.

Sales History browser
The sales history window displays the individual sales history of the title including when and to whom, the quantity and the sale price.

Branch Stock browser
Multi-store users can view the stock figures at other branches. Click on the Show Branch Stock (F8) button.

Navigation and saving changes
Use the Save & Next (F12) and Save & Previous (F9) buttons to scroll through the titles, saving your changes as you go.
Use Save To Browser (F11) button to save your changes and return to the list view.

Finalising a draft purchase order
When your draft order is ready to be finalised, make sure you have the supplier selected in the Draft Purchase Orders window.
Click the Finalise (F12) button to open the Finalise PO window.



This window will show you all the product lines that will be ordered if you finalise the order for this supplier now – i.e. any lines from the Draft PO list that have a non-zero order quantity and are not on hold.

SAN
Note: this field will only be displayed if you have set up an alternate SAN number in System options .It does not affect most users,
Bookscan caters for you trading using two SAN numbers (i.e. you will have two accounts with some of your suppliers, one for each SAN). You can set the two SANs on the Licensee tab of System Options. With two SAN numbers set, the Finalise PO window will display a drop down box where you can select which of your SANs to use on the current purchase order.

PO Number
The PO sequence field shows default purchase order sequence (you can have up to five set in the Purchasing tab of System Options.
The Next PO# field shows the next available purchase order number.

Budget Month
If you are using the budgeting module, you can change the effective month of the order in the Budget Month box.

Special instructions
This is where you can enter instructions to appear on the purchase order.
You have three options:
Select from a list of existing instructions
·In the Special Instructions drop-down list select the required instruction.  
Create a new instruction by editing an existing instruction
·Select an instruction from the drop-down list.  
·Click on the Edit button. This will open the Add field for editing.  
·Amend the Add field as required.  
·When the order is finalised the new instruction will be saved for future use.  
·Note that the existing instruction will not be overwritten - you will just create a new one.  
Add a new instruction
·Type a new instruction in the Add field(such as Backorder if out of stock) that you want to appear on the order.  
·When the order is finalised the new instruction will be saved for future use.  

Delete an instruction
·      Select the instruction you want to delete from the drop down list  
·      Right click in the window, a context menu will apprear with the options Delete PO Instruction & Save Instructions.  
·      Click Delete PO Instruction & then click YES in the Confirmation dialog box that pops up.  

Options
·The Replace checkbox causes to PO instructions hard coded on the PO form, as opposed to adding to these instructions.  
·The Large Font checkbox causes the instructions to be printed in a larger font. Only use this for short messages such as "URGENT", otherwise the full message will not display.  
·The backorder instructions are pre-set instructions to the supplier on how to handle your backorders. The selected instruction will print on the purchase order form.  

Totals
The total box displays the following:
·Lines - the number of lines (products) that will be ordered for this supplier  
·Qty - the quantity of products being ordered.  
·Value - the total cost for the order (you need to take into account that products may have no cost in the title master)  
·Supp min - the supplier minimum order level.  

Print Options
·EDI Orders - if the supplier is setup as an electronic order supplier, this option will be checked. If the supplier is setup as a Pacsteam supplier, this flag will be automatically set. You can, untick the option if you wish to fax/telephone the order instead.  
·Print EDI Orders - if you wish for the electronic orders to print, you can also check this option (it will send electronically, but also print)  
·Copies - if you are printing the order you can select the number of copies to print  
·Print preview – preview the purchase order form on the screen before printing it.  
·Batch Print - this option will cause the purchase order forms to print at the end of processing (when you select the 'Finalise and Close' button) instead of printing the orders as you finalise them.  

Purchase order creation
After you have checked your order in the browser and made any changes to the purchase orders settings, you can create the purchase order.
Note - if you notice that expected lines are missing from the browser, click the Cancel button to return to the Draft PO window. Check to see if those products have a zero order quantity or are set as 'hold'.

To finalise the single order, click Finalise & Close (F12).
If you wish to finalise multiple orders, click Finalise & Next (F11).
If you wish to skip to a different supplier, use the Back (F8) or Skip (F9) buttons.

If you wish to process (finalise) all suppliers orders, check the option ' Process All' and then click one of the finalise buttons.
