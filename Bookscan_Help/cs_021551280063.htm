Kits
Previous  Top  Next

You can group individual products together and sell them as kits (pack). Each kit will have its own unique product code that can be scanned at point of sale or in the Invoice Entry window.
You can choose to have the sales recorded against either the components of the kit or against the kit itself. The latter is the default. You can set kits sales to be recorded against the components in the Sales tab of System Options.
All kits have a binding of KI and a main supplier of ^KIT.

Kits are managed in the Kits window. From the main menu select Purchasing, then Kits.
The upper browser of the Kits window displays all the kits listed in the title master.
The lower browser shows the components of the selected kit in the upper browser.



Creating a kit:  
In the Kits window click on the New Kit (F4) button to open the New Master Record window.
Enter the details into each field. Fields marked with an asterisk are mandatory.
Note: that the binding and main supplier fields have been completed for you.
Click the OK button when done.
You will then be invited to add components to the kit. You can either add them now or do it later
Adding components to a kit:  
You can add components to the currently selected kit in the Add item to kit box.
Scan the barcode of the component into the ISBN field (or use one of the standard shortcuts or click on the F2 title product search button).
After selecting the product a dialogue window will be displayed asking for the quantity of this component in each kit.
Enter this quantity and click OK and the components is added.
Note that if you have already assembled stock against a kit you will not be able to add further components to the kit.

Setting the kit price
You can edit the kit sell price in three places:
·In the New Master Record window when creating the kit.  
·In the title master as you would with any other product.  
·In the Kits window. Here the current sell price is displayed for the current selected kit. Also displayed is the total cost and sell price of all the components. You can use these to assist you in setting a price. To edit the sell price click on the Set Kit Price (F5) button and enter the new sell price.  
Assembling kits  
You can assemble components into kits in the Kits window.
Doing this will reduce the on hand level of the components and proportionally increase the on hand quantity of the kit stock.
The "Able to make" field displays the number of kits you are able to assemble.
Enter up to this number in the Assemble field and then click on the Assemble (F6) button.
Disassembling kits  
The Assembled field displays the number of kits that are currently assembled.
Enter up to this number in the Disassemble field and then click on the Disassemble (F7) button.
Editing a kit  
To change the quantity of a component in a kit, select the component in the lower browser.
Right click on it to open the context (right mouse) menu and select Edit Item.
A dialog box will open where you can change the quantity.
Note: that you can only do this when no stock is assembled for the kit.
Deleting a component from a kit  
To remove a component from a kit, select the component in the lower browser.
Right click on it to open the context menu and select Delete Item.
Note: that you can only do this when no stock is assembled for the kit.
