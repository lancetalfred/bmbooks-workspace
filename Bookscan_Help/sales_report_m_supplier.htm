Sales Report:   Supplier


   Purpose:
Sales overview in selectable periods with imprints focus.  

   Parameters:
From/To Periods, Subtotal-by-Imprint option, Report No-Sales option, Supplier(s) Selection.  

   Output:
Title, ISBN, Cost, On-hand, On-order, MTD Sales, MTD Value and YTD.  
Subtotals by supplier and by imprint (if nominated).  
 

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the report's purpose.  
Subtitle      -   expresses the reporting period.  
lPrintNoSales   -   dictates whether report's second section is included.  
lImprintSubs   -   controls the level of subtotalling.  

See also:   Selection Controls.
