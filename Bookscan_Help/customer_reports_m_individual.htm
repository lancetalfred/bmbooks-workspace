Customer Report:       Individual


   Purpose:
Report purchases for a selected customer.  


   Parameters:
Particular customer.  
Start and finish dates.  
 

   Output:
Title, Author, Department, Purchase-Date, Sale Price, Discount Amount, Quantity, Discounted Price.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
      ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.


See also:   Selection Controls.
