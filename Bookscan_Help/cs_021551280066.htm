Discounting a line
To apply a discount to a line, type the percentage discount (e.g. 10% ) into the Discount field in the edit line.
After you save the line, the discount will be displayed in the Discount column of the browser.

If you do not know what Discount rate you wish to take off an item, but you know the new sell price you wish to give, overtype the Price field with the new amount, then press Tab, this will then calculate the discount for you and set the Price field back to the original amount, and the discount will rate will be put into the Discount field .

Discounting a whole order       
To apply a discount to the whole order, type the percentage discount (e.g. 10% ) into the Discount field in the lower right of the window.
After pressing Tab or Enter the total value will be recalculated reflecting the discount you have entered.
