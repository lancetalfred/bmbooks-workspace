Purchasing Report:      Reprint GRN(s)


   Purpose:
Prints goods received notes, for goods which have not yet been posted into stock.  

   Parameters:
Supplier(s).  

   Output (subject to system settings):
ISBN, Title, Qty, PO#, Inv Cost, Sell Price, Discount and tax components.  
    Subtotals are by supplier and invoice-number.
    Section 2 reports Titles Not Supplied / Unfilled Special Orders.
    Section 3 prints requisition slips.
    Section 4 prints special order slips.
    Section 5 prints (special order) letters to customers.
    Author, Binding and more can be readily added (see Modifying System Reports).
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
cTaxName       -   GST or VAT (used in column-labelling).  
    Also, various flags determining which sections of the report may be omitted.

See also:   Selection Controls.
