Sales Reports

   Select a report below, to view details of selection-options, purpose, output and variables:


   Special:
      Customer:
         All      -   Report all special orders for a nominated customer.
         Single   -   Report status of a specific special order.
         Category   -   Special Orders for customers in nominated category.
         Status    -   Customer's special orders of nominated status.
      All         -   Review the entire Special Orders file.
      Not Found      -   Report Special Order Titles not found.
      Overdue      -   Report on Special Orders not yet filled.
   Daily:
      Graph      -   Graphs the current day's sales over time.
      Cash Book      -   Daily cash book listing.
      All Sales      -   Reports all titles sold today.
      X Report      -   Summarises day's takings.
   Monthly:
      Title Quantities Sold   -   Reports sales' quantities.
      Nett Sales      -   Nett sales figures.
      Commission      -   Monthly salesman commission report.
   History:
      Graph      -   Graphical view of global sales.
      Cash Book      -   Global cash book.
      All Sales      -   Reports all titles sold globally.
      Dept. Levy      -   Levies by department (optionally consolidated).
   Year To Date         -   YTD sales.
   Period         -   All sales in period.
   Category:
      Single Category   -   Detail sales made within a (nominated) category.
      Multiple Categories   -   Compare sales across multiple categories.
   Layby:
      All         -   Outputs entire layby file.
      Overdue      -   Laybys approaching redemption.
      Payments      -   Outstanding layby payments.
   Approvals:
      All         -   Report entire contents of the approvals file.
      Summary      -   Summarise approvals.
      14 Days      -   Report approvals unfilled for specified period.
      Customer      -   Report all approvals for nominated customer(s).
      Reprint Approval   -   Reprint a single approval.
      Apprvl. Return Receipt -   Used by Bookscan (Report Writer can modify it).
   Reprint Invoice/Credit      -   Print (a range of) invoice(s) already posted.
   Reprint Quotation      -   Reprint quotations by either number or customer.
   Department         -   Reports taking by any combination of departments.
   Imprint         -   Sales by imprint by period.
   Best Sellers         -   Best movers' listing.
   Marketing         -   Marketing details.
   Supplier         -   Sales by supplier by period.
   Lifetime         -   Lifetime sales.
   Operator Sales      -   Cash sales by operator (if option system-enabled).
   Vouchers         -   All or outstanding vouchers report.
