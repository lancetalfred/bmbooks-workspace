Creditors - End of Month & Year End
Previous  Top  Next


End of Month
At month end there are three processes to be done on creditors, after your cheque run:
· Print the remittances.  
· Age the accounts  
· Purge old orders.  
 
Remittance Advices
From the main menu, click on Creditors and then point to End of Month and then click on Remittances.
You can choose to print remittances for:
· All Creditors  
· A single creditors (select a creditor in the drop down box).  
· All creditors starting from the one selected.  
· Queued creditors - Use this option if used the Creditors/Cheques/Individual option to record your payments to creditors. Remttance advices for all the creditors you selected will be printed.  

When printing remittance advices for all creditors, check the Print for creditors with current transactions but zero balances
All payments show as current if there is an invoice in the 30 day period and a payment in the current period which clears the account, these would be missed if you don't check this box.

Aging
Before aging the accounts, ensure all activity on the network has ceased.
Then do an accounts back up.
Then age the accounts:
From the main menu, click on Creditors and then point to End of Month and then click on Aging. Click the OK button and it's done.

Purging Old Records
From the main menu, click on Creditors and then point to End of Month and then click on Purge.
The default date is twelve months before the current date.
However, depending on the size of your accounts, choose whatever date suits you.


Year End
The only thing you have to do with creditors at year end is to zero the YTD purchases figures.
From the main menu, click on Creditors and then point to End of Month and then click on Year end.
Change the date if necessary, and then click OK.
