Valuation Report:      Errors


   Purpose:
Locate data errors which can affect stock valuation reports.  

   Parameters:
    Cutoffs (acceptable values) for cost-price, sell-price, on-hand and margin.

   Output:
    ISBN, Title, Author, Sell-price, Discount, Cost, Count. Sorted by department.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
None.
