e-Comms: Configuring Customers
Previous  Top  Next


In e-Comms click on File, then click Setup. (To open e-Comms while in Bookscan click on e-Comms on the main menu)
Next click on the EDI tab.
Enter your e-mail address in the Store EDI Email Address field.
This will allow the recipient of system generated e-mails to identify you as the sender.
e-Comms will automatically dispatch advices according to Customer and Supplier setup.



In Bookscan on the main click on Tables, then click Customer Master.
Select a Customer Master record and open the Address tab.
Enter Customer Email address as shown below.



To save the changes click the Save button.


Then click on the Finance tab.
Then click on the EDI box.
For Wholesale users enter in the EDI number in the EDI field. This is the EDI number given to your customers so you can identify them from one another when they reply to you through EDI.



To save the changes click the Save button.
