Holding stock for a customer
Top 


You can reserve stock for a customer by putting it "on hold". If an item is in hold it must be taken off hold to sell it.

When you go to sell an item on hold, the system will pop-up the Holds window after you scan the item in a sale. Normally this will only occur when all remaining copies are on hold. (However your system may have been set up to have the Holds window pop up whenever there is a held copy of a title).

Putting stock on hold
You can open the Holds window to put a title on hold in two areas:
Title master
On the Details tab, click on the On Hold buttons.
The Holds window will display a list of customer for whom copies of this title are being reserved.
To add a customer to this list, click on the Add Hold Item button and then select the customer from using the Find Customer window.

Customer master
On the Details tab, click on the Holds buttons.
The Holds window will display a list of title held or that customer.
To add a title to this list, click on the Add Hold Item button and then scan the barcode ( or type in the product code) in the window that opens.

Printing a customer docket
When placing an item on hold, you can print a docket for the customer.
To do this ensure the Print checkbox is ticked before putting an item on hold.
Optionally select a "hold to" date. ( This will default to the number of days selected the last time you used this feature).

Releasing held stock
To release an item held for a customer, highlight the customer or title in the list and click the Release Hold Item button.
