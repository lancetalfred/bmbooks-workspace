Academic Report:      Texts


   Purpose:
List text-book requirements applying to (your selection of) courses.  

   Parameters:
Courses, option to omit Dropped texts, optional Page-breaks per course, Year.  

   Output:
Title, Author, Pricings, Semester, Lecturer, suggested quantity.  

 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    lPageBreaks.


See also:   Selection Controls.
