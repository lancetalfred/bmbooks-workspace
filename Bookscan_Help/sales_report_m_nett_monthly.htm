Sales Report:   Monthly Nett Sales


   Purpose:
Overview of monthly sales per title (per department or supplier).  

   Parameters:
Sort-order, Year, Month and (if not sorted by quantity) nominated suppliers or departments.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Sales-quantity, Cost of sales, Nett sales, RRP, On-hand and YTD Sales.  
Subtotalled by either supplier or department.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  


See also:   Selection Controls.
