When the ticket module is enabled, a Ticket button will be displayed in the POS window.



Click on the Ticket button to open the Ticket sales window:


The browser on the left shows all the current shows.
The sessions and ticket prices are shown for show selected in the browser.
Note that the price points 'comps' and 'vouchers' are added by the program.

To sell some tickets:
Select the show.
Select the session,
Overtype the ticket quantities as required.
Click the Save (F12) button.
In the diagram above we are selling two adult and two child tickets to the matinee session of Wind in the Willows.

The sale line will be added to the browser of the POS.
Add any other sales (say from the candy bar).



Then just process it as a standard POS sale - click the Finalise (F12) button to open the Tendering window where you can accept your cash or process an EFTPOS payment. As the sale is completed a sales docket will print on the docket printer and the tickets on the ticket printer.

Options
Barcodes
Instead of using the Ticket button, you can enter the barcode of the show in the Barcode field. The Ticket Sales window will open with the show you scanned already selected. You may find it useful to print barcodes for each of your current shows and stick on a sheet of paper at each POS. Operator can then just can the required show instead of selecting it from the list.

Movie vouchers
You can set up movie vouchers in the Title Master window in a similar way to Bookscan tracked vouchers. The vouchers are good for a set number of shows. Customers can then use these to purchase tickets with the program keeping track of the number of shows left on the voucher.
In the Title master window, create your voucher product. Vouchers should have a barcode for the product and each voucher book should a voucher number unique to the product code. For example you have had printed a set of 1000 ten-ticket vouchers with a barcode of '46466565464' and numbered 1 to 1000. In the New Title window, set the:
·Product Code field to '46466565464'.  
·Title to say, 'Movie voucher – 10 shows'  
·Product Type field to 'Ticket voucher'.  
·Ticket Voucher: Shows field (on the Extra fields tab) to 10.  
·Sell price.  
You can set up as many movie voucher types as you like (e.g. 20-ticket voucher etc). To sell your books of vouchers, scan the barcode in the POS window. In the pop window enter the voucher number and proceed with the sale as usual.
When customers use their vouchers when purchasing tickets through the POS, select the 'Vouchers' price point. A pop up window will be displayed where you can enter the voucher number.

Reservations
If the customer has made a reservation for his tickets, process the sale as usual except that in the Ticket Sales window you should tick the Reserved Seats (F11) check box. When you click the Save (F12) button the Reservation window will open. Select the reservation and proceed as normal. This will cause the reservation to be cleared.



Note that the program will prevent you from selling more seats than are available in the selected theatre for that show. So it is important for popular shows to clear each reservation as the tickets are picked up.
