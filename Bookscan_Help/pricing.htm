Quantity Discounts
Previous  Top  Next

 
Quantity Discounts is where you can set up prices for bulk purchase of a product. If say, you are selling pens at $1.50 each,  
but also sell them in packs of 10 for $12.00, you can set up the pack price here.  
Note that discounted price is only applied for full packs, not for quantities over the pack price. In our example above, buying 15 pens would see 10 charged at $1.20 each and five at $1.50.  
 
Opening the Quantity Discount window  
In the main menu, click on Tables/ Pricing/ Quantity Discounts.  
 
The Quantity Discount Offers window will open.  
 
Adding a quantity discount  
To add a quantity discount to a product, click the Add button and then enter the following details:  
 
ISBN   Scan the barcode or type in the ISBN.  
Quantity   Enter the pack size or discount quantity.  
Sell price   Enter the sell price of the pack. Note - not the      
   price per item.  
Offer starts   If this is a limited offer, insert the start and end  
/ends   dates. If no time limit, leave blank.  
 
Click OK when done.  
 
Other options  
  
Edit   To edit a line, highlight it and click this button.  
   You can change the quantity, price and offer  
   dates.  
 
Delete   Highlight the line you wish to delete and click this  
   button  
 
Hide/Show   Hides/displays offers past their end date.  
Expired  
  
Close   Closes the window .
