Sales Report:   Global Cash Book


   Purpose:
Report cash book over global data.  

   Parameters:
Start and finish dates, Summary option, option to print Banking list.  
 
   Output:
Date, Customer ID, Tender-type, Netts, Discount, Time-stamp, Register, Tax and Operator.  
Subtotalled by transaction type and tender type.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle   -   reflects the selection parameters.  
Summary      -   determines the level of detail reported.  
WithBankList   -   dictates if second section of report should be printed.  
NumInvs      -   informs section 2 of total number of invoiced payments.  
NumCNs      -   informs section 2 of total number of invoices and credit notes.  
NumCash   -   informs section 2 of total number of sales.
