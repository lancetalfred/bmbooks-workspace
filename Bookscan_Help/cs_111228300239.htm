Customer Search
Previous  Top  Next


In many instances, when you open the Customer Master window, the Find Customer window will open above it; so you can immediately search for the customer you want. Otherwise, open the Find Customer window by clicking the Find (F2) button on the Customer Master window.



The search allows for five types of searches:
·Surname - search by the customer surname.     
·Full name – search by the customer name. This is useful for searching for company names.  
If more than one customer fits the surname or name search criterion, the List view of customers will open in alphabetical order from the name you requested. Scroll down the list and click on the one you require. Then click the Detail tab or just double click the customer.  
If the customer has been is identified uniquely, then the Detail view for the customer will open.  
·Customer Code - type in all or part of the customer key.  
·Frequent Shopper key   - scan in the barcode on the customer card.  
·Embedded - This is a powerful search that will look for a word or phrase in several fields of the customer master. Type your search string into the Find field and press the Enter key. The program will search for any occurrence of your string in any of these fields: name, invoice/postal address, main delivery address, short comments, customer abstract (long comments), Pacstream number, email address, phone number, mobile phone number.  
If only one customer meets your search criterion, that customer will be displayed.  
If more than one customer is found, a pop up browser window will be displayed from which you can choose the customer you want.  
So an easy way to find a customer is to type their mobile phone number into the Find Customer window.
