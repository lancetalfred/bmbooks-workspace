Quantity Ordered

This is the quantity to be ordered when you finalise the order.

It is calculated as follows:

Order qty = Minimum stock - (Onhand qty + On order qty)

To change this quantity:
·Highlight the title. Then click on the Edit (F8) button to go to detail view (or just double click on the title.)  
·Move to the Order Qty box.  
·Type in the new figure.  
·Click on the Save to browser (F11) button again to return to list view.
