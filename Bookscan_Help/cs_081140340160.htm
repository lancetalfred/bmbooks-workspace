Using Categories
Previous  Top  Next


Categories allow you to group both titles and customers and can be a very useful marketing tool.
A customer's categories are updated with each sale while a title's categories are manually added, usually at title set up.

Categories and Titles
· Categories can have a variety of uses. They can be used to search for titles, to select titles for say, inclusion in booklists or for use on a web page.

· Titles are manually added to a category either during title set up or in title or category maintenance.

· A title can be in as many categories as you wish.
For example, Kurma's Indian Cooking could be included in the Cooking, Indian Cooking and Vegetarian Cooking categories. It could also be included in the category Christmas 2002 Catalogue.

Categories and Customers
· The categories used for titles can also put against customers to allow you to track their interests. For example Needlework may be used for both titles and customers.

· You can also create categories for use just with customers for say mailouts.
For example, you might have a category called Teachers and another Librarians. You could also have one called Invite which you could use to produce invitations lists.  

· When you enter the Customer key during a cash sale or when creating laybys, invoices and special orders, the system checks the categories of the items purchased, and if they are not already attached to the customer records, adds them.
This feature can be turned on or off in System Options
.

· You can also manually add customers to a category in Category Maintenance.

· A customer can be in as many categories as you wish.
