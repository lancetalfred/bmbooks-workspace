Title Report:         On Hold


   Purpose:
Review title(s) on hold.  

   Parameters:
Minimum On-hold Duration to report, Particular Title/ISBN (or All).  

   Output:
Customer ID, Order Date, Quantity.  
Subtotals by ISBN.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle       -   reflects selected parameters.  
Subtitle      -   reflects selected parameters.
