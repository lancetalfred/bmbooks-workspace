Invoicing - Appending All Special Orders
Top 

This feature of Invoice Entry enables you to quickly invoice all special orders for all customers where stock has been received.
The process loads in turn, into the header, each customer who has special orders where the stock has been received.
The special order lines for the first customer are automatically copied into the browser. You can then finalise the order in the usual way by clicking on the Finalise button. The invoice will then be created and the next customer and their special orders are loaded. Use the Skip button to skip processing any customer.

Processing Details
Header window
·In the Invoice Entry window ensure that you have not selected a customer and that the Transaction Type is set to Standard Invoice.  
·Click on the Start Batch Alt F8 button to begin processing.  
·The number of customers to be processed and the total number of special order lines will be displayed.  
·The first customer with special orders will be loaded.  
·Make any changes you require in the header (e.g. re-set the terms ) and click on the Save F12 button.  

Browser Window
·The browser showing that customer's special orders will now be displayed.  
·Amend or delete any lines you need to and add further lines to the invoice if required.  
·Click on the Finalise F12 button.  

Footer window
·Make any changes or additions you require to the footer window (e.g. add a freight charge) and click on the Post F12 button to create the invoice.  
·The next customer will now be loaded and you can process their orders in the same way.  
·To skip a customer click on the Skip Customer Alt F9 button; the next customer will be loaded in its place.  
·Add any time you can use the End Batch F8 button to stop processing.  
·When the last customer with available special orders is finished, batch mode will end automatically.
