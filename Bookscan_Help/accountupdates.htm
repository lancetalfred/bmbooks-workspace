Customer Account Utilities

Customer account terms
To globally change the customer terms in the customer master, go to the Customer account terms area:
·Select the "from" terms e.g. 14 days.  
·Select the "to" terms e.g. 30 days.  
·Click on the Update Terms button. In the above example all accounts with terms of 14 days will be updated to 30 days.  

Customer account type change
If you are changing accounts from open item to balance brought forward, or the other way around, and there are outstanding transactions on the accounts, then you will need to run this routine to update the existing account transactions.

WARNING: These routines are not completely reversible - so if you change your mind you may have lost some of your data. Please contact Barcode Solutions before proceeding. You should also backup your files.
WARNING: Do not change the account type on the customer master. This routine will reverse whatever the account type setting is there.

·In the Customer account type area, use the radio buttons to select whether to globally change all accounts or to just change a single customer.  
·In the case of a single customer, select the customer from the combo box.  
·Click on the Update Account button.  

Effects of this routine:
Open item to balance brought forward:
·The customer account type will be changed to balance brought forward.  
·Transactions older than 30 days will be moved to the history file. In debtors will only see the current transactions.  

Balance brought forward to open item:
·The customer account type will be changed to open item.  
·Transactions older than 30 days will be totaled for each of 30, 60 and 90 days and a journal added to the account for each of these (where they are non-zero). You will now be able to allocate payments and credits against these debit journals.  

Customer Account Recovery
If the customer account type has been changed and there were outstanding transactions on the account and the above routine was not run, then running month end will cause the account to display anomalous figures. This routine will repair those figures and re-calculate the current, 30, 60 and 90 day figures. If figures are correct then the routine will have no effect.
·Backup of your files.  
·In the Customer Account Correction area, just click on the Recovery button.  
·When asked if you wish to proceed, click Yes button.  
·If there are any discrepancies for any customers a window will display with the current, 30, 60 and 90 day figures and the correct figures for each in error only.  
·To fix, click Repair button
