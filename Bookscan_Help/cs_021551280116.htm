Receiving Payments for Laybys
Top 


Layby payments are recorded in the Layby Enquiry window.
For details of this window click on the link: Layby enquiries.



·From the main menu, click on Sales, then Laybys, then click on Layby Enquire.  
·Select the layby number in the Layby No field or the customer in the Customer field and highlight the required layby in the browser.  
·Click on the Make Payment (F7) button.  
·The Tendering window will open with the total owing displayed in the Layby Deposit field.     
·If required, overtype this field with the payment amount.  
·If the payment is not in cash, select a new tender type.  
·Move to the Amt Tendered field and enter the tender amount.  
·Click the OK button or press the Enter key to complete the transaction.
