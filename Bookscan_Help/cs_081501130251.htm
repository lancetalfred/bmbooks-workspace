The schools that will be used for the pre-pack module will be stored in this section of Bookscan.
From the main menu click Tables,then point to Authority Tables and then click on Schools

Schools
Add
Click the Add button.
Complete the fields.
Code and Name are the only required fields.
Click the OK button.

Edit
Click on the school record and click the Edit button.
Make changes to the fields. Code cannot be changed.
Click the OK button.

Contacts
Contacts are the people at the school that will be contacted when there is any enquires.

Click the Contacts button to display the contacts window and use the following options. Any contacts will be displayed in the list here.

Add Contact
To add new contacts click the Add Contact button.
The new contact will be displayed in the list

Remove Contact
To remove a contact from the school click on the contact and click the Remove Contact button.
Click the Yes button.
The contact will now be removed.

Close
To return to the Schools window, click the Close button.
