Academic - Rolling the course information  
 Top 


Either once a year or at the end of a semester (depending on how you want figures aggregated) you must do a rollover.
A rollover turns this year's figures into last year's and clears the current year for new information

The default dates given are the end of October, to allow next year's enrollments and course alterations to be entered.
However, type in whatever dates are appropriate for your purposes.

Use the right mouse menu in the white browser section and select End of Year Rollover to access this option.
Once that step has completed, use the right mouse menu in the yellow header section and select Update academic sales totals table to update the history totals for the Period Sales Display.
