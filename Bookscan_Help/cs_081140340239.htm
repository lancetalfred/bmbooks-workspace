Parked sales
Top 


A sale can be interrupted and parked for recall later. This might be used if a customer wants to go and find another item part way through the sale or if you wish to complete the sale a different register.

In the Cash Sales window, you can park cash sales, refunds, laybys and invoices. You may park as many transactions as you wish.

Note that when closing sales at end of day all unfinalised parked sales are deleted.

Parking a sale.

To park a sale, just click on the Park Sale button in the Cash Sales window.
(This button is enabled and its caption show Park Sale when at least one line of a transaction has been saved and is displayed in the browser, and the data entry line is empty).

Recalling a park sale.

Whenever there are parked sales waiting to be processed, and you are not currently processing a transaction, the Parked Sale button will be enabled and its caption show Recall Parked.
Every five seconds the system will check for parked sales and if they exist, enable the Recall Parked button. When there are no parked sales, the button will be automatically disabled.

·Click on the Recall Parked button to open the Parked Sales window.  


·The illustration shows that there are three parked transactions - an invoice, a cash sale and a layby.  

· Highlight the transaction you require and click on the Select button. The transaction (including any discounting) will be copied into the browser and removed from parked sales. Then just process the transaction as usual.  

·To delete a parked sale, highlight it and then click on the Delete button.  

To close the window without restoring a parked sale, click on the Exit button.
