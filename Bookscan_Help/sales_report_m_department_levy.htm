Sales Report:   Department Levy

   Purpose:
Reports levies by department and, optionally, by consolidated department.  

   Parameters:
Start and finish dates, Consolidation option,  
 
   Output:
Qty, Discount, Nett Sale and Cost Price (ex GST), Dollar and Percentage GP.  
Subtotalled by department.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the the report's date parameters.  
lConsolidate   -   determines subtotalling style.  

See also:   Selection Controls.
