Sales Report:   Global Sales History Graph


   Purpose:
A graphical view of global sales.  

   Parameters:
Start and finish dates plus a checkbox to include or exclude Invoices from the figures generated.  

   Output:
Solid bar-graph.  


   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
Average       -   average sales per unit time.  
TotVal      -   informs report of average sale value,.  
TotQty      -   informs report of average quantity sold.  
cScale      -   x-axis scaling factor.  

See also:   Selection Controls.
