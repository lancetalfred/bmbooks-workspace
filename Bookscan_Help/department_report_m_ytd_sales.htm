Department Report:       Year-To-Date Sales


   Purpose:
Compare sales and costs by department in two years.  
 
   Parameters:
Year 1, Year 2 and ISO Week (up to which to report).  
 
   Output:
Department name, columns for each year's Sales, Costs and ratios.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  
cYear1   -   first year of the comparison.  
cYear2   -   second year of the comparison.  
lByDept   -   indicates whether the report is being run by Department. It can also be run "by Supplier".
