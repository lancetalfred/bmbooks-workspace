Invoicing a Special Order, Approval
Quotation or Pro Forma  
 Top 

You can invoice a pro forma invoice, quotation, special order or approval in the Invoice Entry window.
·Select the customer  
·Enter the header details and click on the Save button  
·(Note that for approvals you must select the transaction mode Adjust stock (retail module) or Already Sent (wholesale module))  
·Click on the Append button to display the Append combo box.  
·Select the transaction you wish to append.  
·You can append multiple transactions and also add further lines manually.  
·Post the invoice as usual.
