Consignment inwards and outwards is a separately licensed module within Bookscan. Contact Barcode Solutions to purchase a license.
The consignment outwards module is very similar to the approvals module. The main difference is in the way you invoice a consignment.

Creating consignments out
Consignments out are creating in the Order Entry window. Refer the Order Entry chapter for details.

Managing consignments out and consignments out enquiries
You can mange and enquire on consignments out in the Consignments Out Enquiry window. Open this window from the main menu: Sales/ Consignments/ Consignments Out.

Displaying consignments out
You can lookup consignments out in three ways:

Enquiry by consignment number
In the Consignment No field, type in the consignment number and press the Enter key or click on the down arrow and select from the drop down list. All the lines on the consignment will be displayed in the browser.
   
Enquiry by customer
Select a customer in the Customer field in the usual way - i.e. enter the customer code, use the F3 button to open the customer search window or the Alt F3 button to recall the last customer. All consignment lines for the customer will be displayed in the browser.

Enquiry by ISBN
Type the ISBN into ISBN field. Alternatively use one the title shortcuts, click the F2 button to open the product search window or use the Alt F3 button to recall the last ISBN you used in the program. All the consignments out for that ISBN will be displayed in the browser.



The browser will display three quantity fields:
Qty      The quantity ordered.
Ret'd      The number of this item returned by the customer.
Inv'd      The quantity of this consignment line that has been invoiced.

Actions
Deleting a consignment
In the browser, highlight a line of the order you wish to delete and click on the Delete Order (F4) button.

Deleting a line from a consignment
Highlight a line in the browser, click on the Delete Line (F5) button.

Consignment returns
Click on the Returns (F6) button. The window will change to an orange colour and the browser will display only items which can be returned. See consignment returns below for details.

Invoice a consignment
As sales are reported on your customer's consignment stock, you can invoice them by clicking on the Invoice Sales (F8) button. The browser will display in invoicing mode and the window will turn green. See invoicing consignments below for details.

Printing
To view the consignment reports printing options, click on the Printer Icon on the Bookscan toolbar. A pop up box will be displayed showing the available consignment reports. These are:
All consignments out
Prints a report of all outstanding consignments.
Option: Include consignments that have been filled.

Consignments summary
Prints a summary of outstanding consignments.
Options: Sort by customer or by date.

Consignments over 14 days old
Prints a summary of consignments over 14 days old.
Options: Change aging date.
Include consignments that have been filled  

Consignments by customer
Prints a summary report of consignments for selected customers.
Options: Print in title order
Include consignments that have been filled.  

Consignment note reprint
Reprints a single consignment note.
You will need to select the consignment note number from the drop down list.

Consignment returns
Approval returns are done in the Consignments out Enquiry/Returns window. A step by step guide follows:

Step 1:   Enter "returns mode"
·To process returns, click on the Returns (F6) button (or press the F6 key).  
·The window will change to an orange colour to indicate that it is in "returns mode".  
·If you have already selected a consignment number or a customer, then only items eligible for return will be displayed in the browser.  

Step 2:   Select the customer or consignment number
By consignment note number
Select the consignment note number from the drop down box. All items on that consignment that are eligible to be returned will be displayed in the browser.

By customer
Select the customer by entering their key in the Customer field or by using the F3 button. All consigned items for that customer that are eligible to be returned will be displayed in the browser. This option is particularly useful if the customer has taken many items at different times and returns them all in one go.



Browser Column heading definitions
Qty      Quantity originally despatched
Outstand   Number eligible to be returned (i.e. not invoiced or already returned)
Ret Qty   Quantity of returns of that you have entered in this session.


Step 3:   Enter the items to be returned
There are two methods for processing the returns
Scanning
If you have the returned items in front of you, you can scan them in.
·In the Barcode field, scan or type in the barcode.  
·A dialogue box will open asking for the Returns Quantity (defaulting to the quantity that is eligible to be returned).  
·Change the quantity if you wish and click on the OK button (or press the Enter key).  
·Repeat this step for each line to be returned.  
Note: Items entered this way are processed against the oldest consignment number first.

Double clicking
If multiple items have been sent to a customer on different consignment numbers, you may wish to use the double clicking method to ensure that returns are allocated against the correct consignment number.
·Double click on a line in the browser.  
·A dialogue box will open asking for the Returns Quantity (defaulting to the quantity that is eligible to be returned).  
·Change the quantity if you wish and click on the OK button (or press the Enter key).  
·Repeat this step for each line to be returned.  

Step 4:   Options   
·To print a receipt for these returns, check the 'Print returns list' box.  
·To check for special orders, check the Check special orders box. This will cause a Special Orders Slip to print if anyone is waiting for this item.  
·To print a returns docket n the docket printer, tick the Print Docket checkbox.  

Step 5: Process returns
·Once all the returns have been entered for the selected customer or consignment number, you can finalise the returns by clicking on the Process Returns (F6) button.  
·If you change your mind, click on the Cancel Returns button (or press the Escape key) to cancel the returns process.  

Step 6:   End returns mode
·To go back to enquiries, on the Cancel Returns button (or press the Escape key).  


Invoicing consignments out
You can invoice consignments out in the Approval Enquiry window. Open this window from the main menu: Sales/ Consignments/ Consignments Out.

Step 1: Set to invoice mode
After selecting a consignment or customer, click on the Invoice Sales (F8) button.
·The browser will display in consignment invoicing mode and the window will turn green. Note that only consignment lines that have stock still outstanding will be displayed.  
·If you see a blank browser, then either the consignment has been invoiced or returned in full.  

Step 2: Select input method

Handy hint  
Auto Scroll (F11)  
Tick this checkbox to have the edit line display the next line when you click the Save Line (F12) button. Using this option can speed up data entry.  

The program provides two methods for entering your customer's sales data:
Sales quantity method
Use this method if your customer has reported his actual sales quantities.
·Click on the Sales radio button to set this mode of processing.  
·Double click on a line in the browser that you want to invoice.  
·The edit line will appear showing the ISBN, sales quantity field and the pricing.  



·The sales quantity will default to zero except when you are editing a line, when it will show the quantity you entered previously.  
·Type in the sales quantity.  
·Amend the pricing and discount fields as required.  
·Click on the Save Line (F12) button to save the line.  
·Alternatively click the Clear Edit Line button (or press the Escape key) to close the edit line without saving.  
·In the browser the quantity to be invoiced will display in the Sales column.  
·Repeat steps 2 and 3 for each line you wish to invoice.  

Stock count method
Use this method if your customer has reported the amount of stock he has left.
·Double click on a line in the browser that you want to invoice.  
·The edit line will appear showing the ISBN, stock count field and the pricing.  



·The stock count field will default to the quantity outstanding except when you are editing a line, when it will show the quantity you entered previously.  
·Type in the stock count.  
·Amend the pricing and discount fields as required.  
·Click on the Save Line (F12) button to save the line.  
·Alternatively click the Clear Edit Line button (or press the Escape key) to close the edit line without saving.  
·In the browser the stock count will display in the stock count column and the (calculated) sales quantity will display in the Sales column.  
·Repeat steps 2 and 3 for each line you wish to invoice.  

Step 3: Create invoice
To create the invoice, click on the Post Invoice (F8) button. Alternatively click on the Cancel Invoicing button (or press the Escape key) to cancel the process and return to enquiry mode.
The effects of posting are:
·An invoice will be created.  
·Both the stock on hand and consignment quantities in the title master will be reduced for each line.  
·The quantity invoiced in the consignments out database will be increased for each line.
