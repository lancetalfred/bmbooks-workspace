Sales Report:   Reprint Invoice/Credit Range   
         Command-line execution enabled.
   Purpose:
Print (a range of) invoice(s) which have already been posted.  

   Parameters:
Low and high invoice-references in range (leaving low reference blank will print just one invoice).  
    Option to bypass the print-preview.
 
   Command-line Parameters:   
    /INV_REP /<WantPreview> /<StartingInvoiceNo> /<LastInvoiceNo>

   INV_REP      -   Identifies the report to be run.
   WantPreview      -   /Y gives a preview, /N avoids it, /? will let the user choose.
   StartingInvoiceNo   -   Low (or only) invoice-number in range to be printed.
   LastInvoiceNo   -   May be omitted to print only one invoice, else last in range.

   Output:
Conventional invoicing details.
