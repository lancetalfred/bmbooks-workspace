Debtor Report:         Journals


   Purpose:
    View all debtor journals (debit and credit) within a nominated period.

   Parameters:
    Option to restrict transactions to those within the current period.

   Output:
    Debtor ID and Name, Transaction identifiers, Sundries, Sales-tax, Freight and Amount.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle      -   Reflects selection criteria.




See also:   Selection Controls .
