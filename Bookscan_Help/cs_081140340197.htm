Recording a payment
Step 1:
From the main menu, click on Creditors and then click on Transactions.

Step 2:
In the Creditors Transactions window, select a supplier by either:
· typing the supplier key in the Supplier box.  
· clicking the button and scrolling through drop  
down list, or  
 
· clicking the button and using the Find window.  

Step 3:
Right click anywhere in the window and select Payment from the drop down menu.

Step 4:
Enter a Reference number (say the cheque number), the Transaction amount and a Description of the transaction.
Click OK when done.

    Return to accounts payable summary
