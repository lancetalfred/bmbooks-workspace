Deleting a title

Titles can be deleted through the Title Master individually, but with a restriction: all stock figures must be zero. This includes stock currently on hand, on order, and on consignment, minimum stock etc. (but does not include the last stocktake figure)
To delete titles:
·Open the Title Master.  
·Search for the title you wish to delete in the search window.  
·When found, ensure the title is selected (either by having the title open in the 'Details' tab, or highlighted in the 'List' tab).  
·Click the Delete Icon (red with a white cross) on the toolbar at the top of the Bookscan window or press the F5 key.  
·You will be asked to confirm the deletion and then given an option to move the title to the archive.  
 
Note: All quantities must be zero for the title to be deleted.
