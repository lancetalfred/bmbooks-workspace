Other Reports:         Promo Titles


   Purpose:
List the titles in a nominated promotion.  

   Parameters:
Promotion, Sort-order preference.  

   Output:
    NOID, Code, Title, Author, Supplier, Department, promotional and normal Cost/Sells, Onhand, Margin, New flag.
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle   -   shows the selected promotion.
