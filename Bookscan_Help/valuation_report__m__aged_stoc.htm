Valuation Report:      Aged Stock

   Purpose:
To vet stock that is on-hand and hasn't sold. Optionally marks down cost and/or sell prices.  

   Parameters:
    Sort-option, start and finish dates. This report can also mark down sell and/or cost prices.

   Output:
    ISBN, Title, Last receipt date, Onhand, Cost and Sell prices with extended values + mark-downs.
    Sort by supplier or department.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    Subtitle      -   Reports any mark-down values employed.
    lBySupplier   -   Sort-order flag.
    CostMarkRate   -   The mark-down rate used against Cost price.
SellMarkRate   -   Mark-down rate for Sell price.
    dStart      -   The earliest date for the report. (See next.)
    dEnd      -   The latest date for the report. The report assumes that all stock on-hand                   was received on the master-file's (single) record of last date received.

See also:   Selection Controls.
