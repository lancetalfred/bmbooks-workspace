Debtor Report:         Master List


   Purpose:
    Print the master list of debtors.

   Parameters:
    Option to sort by salesman.

   Output:
    Debtor Key, Name, Contact, Address and Phone.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle    -   Reflects selection criteria.
    SalesSort      -   Controls appearance of subgroupings.



See also:   Selection Controls.
