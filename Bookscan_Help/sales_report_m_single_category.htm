Sales Report:   Single Category


   Purpose:
Review sales details within a nominated category (uses title's first category).  

   Parameters:
Category, date-range.  
 
   Output:
ISBN, Title, Quantity, Cost-of-sales, Nett, RRP, On-hand, Profit.  
Sorted by sales quantity.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle   -   reflects the selection parameters.  
    Subtitle      -   full name of the selected category.
