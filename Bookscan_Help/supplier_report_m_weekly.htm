Supplier Report:       Weekly


   Purpose:
    Compare up to nineteen weeks of sales by supplier (across current and prior year).

   Parameters:
Starting week.  

   Output:
Supplier name, 19 columns - each reflecting one week of sales.  


   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle   -   reflects the selection parameters.  
cStartWeek   -   determines labelling of columns.  
cLastYear   -   first year of the comparison.  
cThisYear   -   second year of the comparison.  
lByDept   -   indicates whether the report was run by Supplier or by Department.  


See also:   Selection Controls.
