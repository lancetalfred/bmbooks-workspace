Sales Report:   Period Sales


   Purpose:
Report sales within a given period.  

   Parameters:
Cutoff quantity and Period for reporting.  

   Output:
Title, Author, On-hand, Last Sale-date, 12 columns of period sales quantities plus one totalling column.  


   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  
Cutoff      -   the minimum level of sales reportable.  

See also:   Selection Controls.
