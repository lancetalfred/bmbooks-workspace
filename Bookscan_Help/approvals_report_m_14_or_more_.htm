Approvals Report:   14 Or More Days Unfilled


   Purpose:
Report all approvals older than a nominated date (defaults to 14 days).  

   Parameters:
Cutoff date, option to omit approvals which have been filled.  
 

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Quantities, Prices, Approval-date, Days outstanding, Customer name and phone.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
      ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection.



See also:   Selection Controls.
