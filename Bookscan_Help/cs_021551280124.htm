Invoice, EDI and picking slip enquiries

In the Invoice Enquiry window you can look up:
·invoices,  
·credit notes,  
·EDI orders,  
·outstanding picking slips (distributor/publisher configuration) and  
·customer backorders (distributor/publisher configuration).  

Open the window from the main menu: Sales/Invoices/Enquiry.



Selecting the enquiry type
The default enquiry type is invoice and credit note. To change the enquiry type, click on the appropriate button – EDI Orders (F7), Pick Slips (F8) and Backorders (F9).
Notes
·Picking slips and backorders are only relevant for distributor/publisher configurations.  
·Back orders and EDI Orders are only available during customer searches. Select the customer first, and then click on the EDI Orders (F7) or Backorders (F9) button  
·Credit notes display with negative figure in the Qty column of the browser and a red text field saying "credit". See the diagram below.  



You can display transactions by transaction number or by customer code.
Displaying a single transaction
(See the top diagram)
To display at transaction in the browser, select it from the Trans No combo box. The easiest and fastest way to do that is type in the transaction number and press the Enter key. Alternatively use the mouse to select it from the drop down list.

Displaying a customer's transactions.
Select a customer in the Customer field in the usual way: entering the customer code or pressing the F2 button to open the customer search window.



In customer mode, you can opt to display the customer's outstanding EDI orders, picking slips and backorders by clicking the corresponding button.

Searching for a transaction by customer order number
Click on the Order No (F11) button to open the Order No Search pop up window. Enter the order number onto the search field and press the OK button. The program will search the invoice and picking slip database tables and if it finds the transaction, display it in the browser.

Options

Changing the backorder quantity (wholesale module only)
·Select the customer in the Customer field.  
·Click on the Backorders (F9) button.  
·Double click on the line you want to change.  
·A dialogue window will open displaying the existing backorder quantity.  
·Overtype the new quantity and click OK the button.  

Deleting backorders
·In backorder view,highlight the lines you want to delete.  
·Click on the Delete Item (F5) button.  

Deleting a picking slip
Single transaction view
·Set the transaction type to picking slip by clicking on the Pick Slips (F8) button.  
·Select a picking slip from the Trans No combo box.  
·Click on the Delete Order (F4) button.  

Customer view
·Select the customer in the Customer field.  
·Highlight a line from the order you want to delete.  
·Click on the Delete Order (F4) button.  
·Note that you can only delete one picking slip at a time.  

Deleting lines from a picking slip
·In picking slip view,highlight the lines you want to delete.  
·Click on the Delete Item (F5) button.  

Amending picking slips
·You can make the following amendments to picking slips:  
oforward order date,  
oheader discount,  
ofreight.  
·Type your changes in the appropriate fields and then click on the Save (F12) button.  


Quotation and pro forma invoice enquiries
Pro forms invoices and customer quotations are created in the Order Entry window. They can be viewed in the Pro form/Quotation Enquiry window. Open the enquiry window from the main menu: Sales/Invoices/Quotes/pro formas.



In the browser, pro forma invoices show a tick in the "Pro F" column.
