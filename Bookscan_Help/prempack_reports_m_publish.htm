Pre-pack Report:      Publish


   Purpose:
    Lists a nominated school's booklist, by nominated supplier(s).

   Parameters:
School, Supplier(s).  

   Output:
Supplier, ISBN, Title, Imprint and Pricing information.  
   
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    cSchoolName   -   Name of the nominated school.


See also:   Selection Controls.
