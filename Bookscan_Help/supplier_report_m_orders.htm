Supplier Report:       Orders


   Purpose:
    Report orders by supplier.

   Parameters:
None  

   Output:
Supplier name, 12 columns of monthly orders' totals plus a totalling column.  


   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle -   reflects the selection parameters.  
Summary   -   determines the level of detail reported.  
cType   -    determines what sort of transactions the report will display.  
lMovement -   determines if the report is concerned with stock movements generally.  
lOrders   -   determines if the report is focussing on orders.  
lAbs   -   formats certain output either with or without sign (+ or -).  


See also:   Selection Controls.
