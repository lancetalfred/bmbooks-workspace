Receiving Stock - EDI Invoices
Previous  Top  Next

EDI invoices are electronic copies of your suppliers' invoices that you would normally receive on paper. The advantage of soft copy invoices is that you can receive your stock without keying the data. This saves time and improves accuracy.

To use Bookscan's EDI facilities you must have a Pacstream account. See the EDI chapter for details. Once you have signed up for the Pacstream service, many of your suppliers will forward their invoices to you via Pacstream. Bookscan e-Comms will interface with Pacstream and download the invoices. The EDI invoices can be viewed in the EDI invoices window which is opened from the main menu: Purchasing/ Incoming/ EDI Invoices.



The upper (invoice) browser lists the header information for all outstanding EDI invoices. The lower (lines) browser displays the lines of the invoice selected in the upper browser.

Processing an EDI invoice receipt
·Select the invoice in the Invoice browser.  
·Click on the Scan Stock (F11) button to open the pop up scanning window.  
·Scan the barcode of the stock. Change the quantity if required. (Alternatively have the Singles (F7) checkbox ticked and just scan the barcode of each product).  
·If you receive more than the invoiced amount of any product, the program will prompt you before you save the line. If you scan a product not on the invoce, the program will display a warning message.  
·To edit the quantities or to set stock as received damaged etc, double click on the either the Qty, Rec or To Go column in the lower browser. The following window will open where you can edit the quantities.  
 
·As a shortcut, you can select "Receive all this invoice" from the lower browser's context menu. All lines of the sleected order will be set to the invoice quantity.  
·If you don't want to enter the stock at all, just tick the box marked "Don't scan stock", then click the Receive (F8) button.  
·Once all items are received, click the Receive (F8) button. The receipt will be loaded into the received stock file.  
·The program will offer to open the Receiving window where you can post the receipt.  
·Click OK and the Receiving window will open in list view with your new receipt loaded.  
·Make any amendments here, print the GRN and post into stock as usual.  



Identifying suppliers
Pacstream uses SANs to uniquely identify suppliers. When Bookscan e-Comms is retrieving the invoice from Pacstream, it matches the SAN with SANs stored in the supplier master. Where a match is made the supplier code is displayed in the Supp column of the Invoice browser. Where you haven't yet set the SAN against the supplier, this column will display '****'.



To correct this, identify the supplier concerned, open the Supplier Master window and enter the SAN there. Then use the right mouse to open context menu in the EDI Invoices window and select 'Update suppliers', as shown in the diagram above. The Supp column will now display the supplier code and you can now process that invoice.

Setting prices
In the lines browser, the 'Cost' and 'Retail' columns are the prices quoted on the supplier invoice. The Sell column displays your sell price from the master file. Whether the program automatically updates your sell price when you post your receipt into stock, depends on the setting of this supplier's 'No auto calculations' flag.
Whether you set that flag will depend on your work practices:
·If you have set your sell prices already, set the supplier's 'No auto calculations' flag to true.  
·If you are setting prices as you receive the stock, set the flag to false and set the appropriate pricing calculation. This will cause the sell price to be calculated when the EDI invoice is loaded into the Receiving window.  

Unknown product codes
If a product on an EDI invoice is not in your database, the Title column will display in the lines browser as "Item not on master file". You will need to add the title to the database to be able to receive it.



To add the product, use the right mouse to open context menu in the EDI Invoices window and select 'Add selected ISBNs', as shown in the diagram above. The program will first search the archive, and if it finds the product there re-load it into the master. Next the program will do a web search (Title Page, Nielsen Bookdata or Global BIP) to find the title. Alternatively you can just add the title manually through the New Title window.

Viewing Processed EDI Invoices

You can view processed EDI invoices by ticking the Show Processed checkbox on the EDI Invoices window. When the box is ticked the browsers will display both processed and unprocessed invoices. Processed invoices will display a tick in the 'P' Column.
