Adding Pictures To A Report

Once your report has been loaded into the report-designer, select Drawing Tools (from the Tools menu). Continue the menu-selection with Picture. Drag out a suitably-sized rectangle onto the report, using this tool, and when you release the mouse-button you will be presented with a dialogue enabling you to identify the picture's sort and whereabouts. The currently accepted picture-file formats are: BMP, PCX, PNG, JPG and TGA

More details are available within ReportPro's Help, under "Drawing Tools".
