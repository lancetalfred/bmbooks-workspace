Stocktake Report:      Stocktake-count Errors


   Purpose:
    Report stocktake-counts outside a nominated range (trap data-entry errors).

   Parameters:
    Maximum and minimum counts deemed acceptable.

   Output:
    ISBN, Title, Author and Stocktake count (subtotalled by Department) .

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    None.
