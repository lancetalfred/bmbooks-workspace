Debtors - Year End
Previous  Top  Next


· At the end of the financial year you should make and keep an annual accounts backup.

· You also need to do an annual rollover. This will zero the accumulated year to date figures for each account.

· Before doing this, ensure you have printed all reports

· The Year End routine should be done at the same time as (either just before or after) the end of month. The monthly rollover is still necessary.

· Statements must have been printed before running the year end routine (because it changes the YTD figure).

· The Year End routine is run from the main menu::
· From the main menu click on Debtors Utilities/Year End.  
· Click on the Yes button.  

· If you are short of disk space, you can purge old debtor transaction.
   · From the main menu click on Debtors Utilities/Debtor Purge.
· Select the cut- off date and then click on the Yes button.
