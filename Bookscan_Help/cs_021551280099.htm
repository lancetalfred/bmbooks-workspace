Purging Old Special Orders
Previous  Top  Next


Periodically you should remove old, completed special orders from the database:
·Open the Purge Special Orders window from the main menu: Sales / Specials / Purge.  
·In the displayed window, select the date from which to purge filled special orders.  
·Click the OK button. You will be asked to confirm before the program will perform the deletions.  
·Only special orders which have been finalised will be deleted.
