Check outstanding approvals
Stock out on approval or consignment is counted as part of your inventory and so should be reported as accurately as possible. The system will add all live approvals and consignments to your stocktake counts when finalising the stocktake. To ensure your records are accurate, print a summary report of outstanding approvals and consignments and check that your data is up to date.

From the main menu, select Sales / Approvals / Print.

For a detailed report,
·Click on All,  
·Ensure the Include approvals that have been filled box is unchecked.  
·Click OK.  

For a shorter report,
·Click on Summary  
·Chose the Sort by Date option from the drop down list.  
·Click OK.  

Review the report and delete, return or invoice approvals which are not current.
Note that deleting an approval returns the item to stock.

If you are using the consignment out module, repeat all the above for consignment stock.

Quarantine finalised returns to supplier
·If you have damaged or faulty items waiting to be returned, or any approved returns, finalise them and despatch them to the supplier. This saves having to count them.  
·Cut off processing returns inwards at least a day ahead of the stocktake.  
·Separate finalised and unfinalised returns.  
·Finalised returns must not be included in the stocktake. If you cannot despatch them now, quarantine them so that they are not counted.  
·Returns to supplier which have not been finalised must be counted.  

Quarantine unreceived stock
·Cut off processing goods inwards at least a day ahead of the stocktake.  
·Goods received after this time must be quarantined and not counted in the stocktake.  
·You should also clear any parked receipts in the Receiving window.  
·Ensure all stock received before the cut off time is processed, put away and posted.  
·In the 'list' view of the Receiving window, check for goods that have not been posted into stock. Before the stocktake can commence, this list must be emptied. Investigate each line and either post them or delete them.  

Check that all stock is barcoded
To ensure that the stock counting proceeds smoothly and that it is accurate, it is imperative that all stock is barcoded. Counting stock without barcodes is very slow and very prone to error.
Well in advance of the stocktake, check your stock. Print and attach barcode labels for any items that require them.
