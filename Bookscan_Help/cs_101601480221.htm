Academic Module - Reports  
 Top 


Reports can be accessed from the right mouse menu on the Academic window or from Bookscan reports (select Bookscan Reports/Other/Academic) on the main menu.
All of the reports which focus on texts include an option to exclude dropped texts. The available reports are:

Course
   A listing of courses sorted by faculty, including contact details and ordering summaries.

Texts
   A listing of texts sorted by course, including a summary of recent ordering.

Lecturer
   A listing of courses taught by each lecturer.

Department
   A listing of courses (and, within courses, of texts) by department
   NOTE: "Department" in this context refers to Academic Departments within the institution, not Bookscan/Bookscan Departments.

Book-list
   A simplified listing of texts on a course, with filtering by Year and Semester.
