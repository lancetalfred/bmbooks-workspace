Sales Report:   Lifetime


   Purpose:
Longrange sales-figures.  

   Parameters:
Number of years to cover plus department or supplier on which to focus.  

   Output:
Title, ISBN, Sell-Price, Lifetime Profit and Sales, a column for each year covered, YTD, On-hand and On-order.  
Subtotals by supplier and by imprint (if nominated).  
 

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the report's purpose.  
nCW      -   width of variable columns.  
Col<n>      -   column header (for each of the variable columns).  
C2<n>      -   2nd line of column header (each variable column).  

See also:   Selection Controls.
