Sales Report:   Special Order By Customer Category


   Purpose:
Report the status of special orders for customers in a nominated category.  

   Parameters:
Category, option to include/exclude filled orders.  

   Output:
Letterhead, Order-date, Title, Author, Quantity outstanding, Comments.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
Licensee       -   Name and all variables starting with "L_" (address data).
