Sales Report:   Quotations


   Purpose:
Reprint a quotation.  

   Parameters:
Quotation Number (optionally first narrowing the range by Customer).  

   Output:
Customer, Quotation-number, Validity-date, ISBN, Title, Quantity, Sell-price and Extended price.  

    Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    CustName -   Customer's name.
    dValid   -   The date to which the quotation remains in force.
    nQTNo   -   The selected Quotation number.

See also:   Selection Controls.
