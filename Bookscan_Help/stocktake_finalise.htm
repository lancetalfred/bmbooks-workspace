Finalising the Stocktake
Previous  Top  Next


Once you are satisfied with your count you are ready to complete the stocktake.

Backup your system
First, back up the database. This is essential to guard against catastrophic equipment failure.
From the main menu, click on Utilities/Database Utilities/Backup Data Files.
·This will open the backup window.  
·The backup will be saved to the data directory. The previous backup will be overwritten. To save to another drive select the drive from the combo box.  
·Click on the Backup button.  
·All your data files and your reports will be zipped up and saved as the file ebbackup.zip. This may take some time depending on the size of your database.  
·Click on Exit when done.  

Finalise the stocktake
Open the Stocktake Finalising window from the main menu: Modules/ Stocktake/ Finalise.
In the window that opens, click the OK button.
The system will open the master file and overwrite the stock on hand figures with the stock count figures.
The stocktake is now completed. You can now resume your normal activities.
