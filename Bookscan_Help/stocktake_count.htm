Stocktake - Counting the Stock at a Terminal
Previous  Top  Next

Counting stock can be done using portable barcode scanners or at a PC running Bookscan.  

Counting the stock at a terminal
Stock counting is done in the Count Stock tab opened from the main menu: Modules/ Stocktake/ Stocktake.




Select the Default Department from the drop down box (say STK Stocktake). Products without departments are allocated to this department. After the stocktake you can print a listing of these titles and correct them.

Click on the [F5] toggle button to switch between SINGLES, entering one copy and QUANTITIES, entering multiple copies.
·For single copy scans, every time a barcode is scanned the count is incremented by one. No need to key a quantity - scan the barcode and it's counted.  
·For entering multiple copies, scan the barcode. The barcode will be displayed and the cursor will move to the Quantity text box. Overtype the quantity and press the Enter key.  

Correcting Errors
If you have entered an incorrect quantity, used a wrong ISBN or counted something twice, you can reverse the entry by entering a negative quantity:
·Ensure that you are in multiple copy mode.  
·Scan the barcode of the item you wish to correct.  
·Type in a negative Quantity (eg -6) and press the Enter key.  
Note that the minus sign will not appear until after you type the first digit.

Stocktake Entry browser
The browser shows the titles already counted this session. The quantity fields are:
·Progress   Cumulative total counted so far for this title.  
·On Hand   Quantity showing as currently in stock.  
·Qty      Number added to the count at that time.  

Note: You can enter and exit the system as often as you like during a stocktake. The stocktake begins when you do a Prepare and ends when you've done a Finalise. The count is stored in the Title Master not in the stocktake window.
However when re-opening the Stocktake Entry window, previous processing will not be displayed. So ensure you know what you last counted before losing the window. The window can be closed, Bookscan & all PCs shut down. When they are turned on again, the stocktake will continure from where you left off.
