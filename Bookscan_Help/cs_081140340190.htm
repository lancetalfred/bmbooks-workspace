Customer Enquiry
Step 1: Opening
From the main menu click on Debtors and then on Transactions.
The Debtor Transaction window will open.

Step 2: Selecting a customer
Select a customer in the Customer field either by entering the customer code or using back slash or other shortcuts.
Alternatively click on the Seek (F3) button to open the customer Find window.
To recall the last customer, click Recall Last (Alt + F3) button.



Step 3: Transaction history
Click on the Show History (F9) button to view completed transactions.
Click on the Show Transactions (F9) to return to viewing current transactions.

Note: Current Transactions window is green, as shown in the picture above, whereas the Debtors History is violet colour.

Step 4: Invoice enquiry
To view a transaction's details, double click on the transaction line.
This will display the Invoice Enquiry window. For information on Invoice Enquiry Window, click link.


Invoice Search
If you know the Invoice number, but not the customer it belongs to you can use the Invoice Search.
Click on the Invoice Search (F8) button.
Enter in the Invoice No. i.e. 23.
Then click OK button.

If the invoice number exists, then the customer whom it belongs to will be displayed and the invoice selected.

Context Menu

If you right click to bring up the context menu, there are useful functions shown.


1.Invoice               Create an invoice record if one is missing. See also Search missing invoices..  
2.Credit               Create a credit note record if one is missing.  
3.Payment               Make a Debtor payment.  
4.Debit Journal            Create a journal record to balance a excess credit.  
5.Credit Journal            Create a journal record to balance a excess debit or forgive a debt.  
6.Refund               Refund an over payment  
7.Credit allocation            This opens the credit allocation window to assign unallocated credits or payments to invoices.  
Show Current / History          As per [F9] button.
Recalculate Customer account totals   Recalculates the totals for the selected customer displayed in the top right of the window for Current / 30 / 60 / 90.
Recalculate totals for all customers   As above but for all customers.
Customer account utilities         Open the Account Utilities window.
Search missing invoices         Searched the invoice tables for customer invoices that do not have a matching debtor record.
Compare Invoice total and Debtor Amount Looks fot cases where the invoice total does not match the invoice value recorded in the debtors table.
