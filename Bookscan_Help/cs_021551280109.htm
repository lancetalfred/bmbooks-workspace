Enquiries - Sales History Reports  
 Top 


Step 1:   
From the main menu, click on Sales, then point to History and then click on the report you require.

Step 2:   
Click on the links below for details of the reports.

Reports
Click on the link for details of each report.

Graphic   A graph of sales against time.
      
All sales   A report of items sold (not of payments received),
   grouped by either department or supplier.

Cash Book   A summary of takings for each transaction type
   (sales, layby deposits, etc), each type broken
   down by tender type.
   
Dept Levy   Reports levies by department and, optionally,
   by consolidated department.

Step 3:   
Select the start and finish dates for your report.

Select your parameters and then click OK to print the report.
