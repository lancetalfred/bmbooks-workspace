Sales Report:   Multiple Categories


   Purpose:
Compare sales totals across multiple categories (uses title's first category).  

   Parameters:
Categories, date-range, option to sort by quantity or profit.  
 
   Output:
Category, Quantity, Nett Sales, Nett Cost, Gross Profit.  
Sorted by quantity or profit.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle   -   reflects the selection parameters.  
    SubTitle      -   Expresses sort-criterion.
