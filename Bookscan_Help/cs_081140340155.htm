To add a customer to the customer master, click on the New (F11) button on the Customer Master window. The New Customer window will open:



The fields are similar to the fields in the Customer master window. Some fields that may need more information:
·Customer key field - enter the first five letters of the surname or company name. (Your Systems Option setting may allow more). The program will allocate a number to make the key unique.  
·Full name field - as enter the first name and surname, the name field will be built. For companies, enter the company name in the Surname field and leave the other name fields blank.  
·Finance (F4) button – for debtor customers, click on the Finance (F4) field to display more fields.
