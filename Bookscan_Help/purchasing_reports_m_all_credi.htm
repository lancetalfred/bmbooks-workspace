Purchasing Reports:         All Credit Claims


   Purpose:
Reprint a chronological range of credit claims.  

   Parameters:
Date-range values, Option to use summary format.  

   Output:
Claim No and Date, ISBN, Title, Author, Qty Returned, Return costs, RRPs and GST.  
Subtotalled by Credit Claim No and Supplier.
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle   -   Reflects the selection parameters.   
    lSummarise    -   Determines level of output detail.

See also:   Selection Controls.
