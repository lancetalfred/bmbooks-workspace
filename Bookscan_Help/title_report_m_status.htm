Title Report:         Status


   Purpose:
View title details (by status).  

   Parameters:
Report Type, Specific Type, Sort Order, Statuses to Include.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Author, Imprint, Dept; Supplier, Binding, Invoice Cost, Sell Price and Year of Publication.  
Subtotals by department.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle       -   reflects selected parameters.  
Subtitle      -   reflects further selection criteria.  
HasSubGroups   -   determines whether or not group headings are shown.  


See also:   Selection Controls.
