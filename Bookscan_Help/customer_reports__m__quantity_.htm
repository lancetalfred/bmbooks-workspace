Customer Reports:      Quantity / Cost


   Purpose:
Report the quantity/cost of purchases for selected customer(s).  

   Parameters:
Customer(s), Start and finish dates, Sort-order.  
 

   Output:
Product, Description, Quantity, Cost.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
      ReportTitle       -   reflects the selection parameters.
    Subtitle      -   expresses the nominated sort-order.


See also:   Selection Controls.
