Email Statements Setup
Previous  Top  Next

Statements can be emailed to debtors during End Of Month. Those who do not have an email will be saved into a single PDF that can be printed for mailing.

1. Before setting up in Bookscan, you need to have the PDFexport printer device installed on your computer. To do this, download the installer from http://support.e-web-site.com/downloads/ftp/Barcode_PDF_Writer.zip
Open the zip file with File Explorer and extract the Barcode PDF Writer folder to your Bookscan Downloads folder. Go into the folder & double click the Setup.exe to run the installer.

Follow the prompts & leave the default settings. If asked, do not set it as your default printer.

2. Set up email program.

2a. Mozilla Thunderbird (as at v78.6.1): Go to Tools – Options, Scroll down & click Config Editor.
In the search bar enter mapi.blind-send.warn, double click on it to edit & set it to false.

2b. MS Outlook: You will probably need to apply some settings in the registry to allow Bookscan to send the emails. You can download & set them by running the registry file from: http://support.e-web-site.com/downloads/utilities/Outlook_v12-16.reg

3. In the Customer Master, there are now options to apply a secondary email address for finance and the option for No email statements. If a Finance email is saved on the address tab, it will be used in place of the regular email.



If the client does not wish to receive statements by email, you can tick the No email statements on the Details tab.



4. To set up to email statements, go through the End Of Month process as normal until you reach the Run Statements section. Before running them, tick the I Would Like To Email Statements checkbox & click on the Email Settings button.



In the next window, enter the text you want to appear as the email header, & in the body.



Clicking on the Help For Email Setting button will open a window with the codes to have the emails customised for the clients with their names.



eg. If in the example above, I have used 'Dear &(salute) &(surname),'. This will start the body of the email to, (for example Mr John Smith), as 'Dear Mr Smith,', based on the data entered in the salutation & surname fields of the client in the Customer Master.

Once this has been entered, click the Test button to verify that the PDF printer & email are set up & working. Then click Save (F12).

You can now click Run Statements. This will generate the emails & send them one by one through your email program as an attachment. The emails will go to the clients from the local PC email account, with the normal footer for that account.

Statements that are not emailed will be saved as a PDF in the [database folder]\debeom folder, and can be printed for mailing in bulk if required. After sending emails out, you can see them in your Sent folder, & you should check your Inbox for any rejections or bounce messages. These will come with a reason for the rejection that can be used to correct the issue.
