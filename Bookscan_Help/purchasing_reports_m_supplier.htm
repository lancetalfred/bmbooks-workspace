Purchasing Report:      Supplier


   Purpose:
Report outstanding purchase orders with nominated supplier.  
    Or does the same for Promotional orders.

   Parameters:
Supplier.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Author, Order Date, Order Quantity, Last Invoice Costs, RRP and Sell Price, PO Comments.  

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  
Summary      -   determines the level of detail reported.  


See also:   Selection Controls.
