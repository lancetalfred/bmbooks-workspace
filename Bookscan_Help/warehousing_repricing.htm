Re-pricing backorders
Previous  Top  Next


You can arrange to have the backorders automatically re-priced to reflect the latest prices. This is a very handy feature when you want to take orders on a new title but don't yet have a price for it. It is also useful where you are bringing in products from overseas and, because of fluctuating exchange rates, you don't want to set a price until the stock arrives

Summary
·A title's re-price flag is set to true in the title master.  
·Backorders are taken against the product with the customer's discount set.  
·When the items come into stock, the price is set in the title master.  
·Then when the backorder is released, the pricing on the orders will be calculated based on the new price and the discount set when the order was taken..  

Detailed steps
Title Master window
In the Extra Fields tab of the Title Master window, tick the Re-price field .
If you wish, you can also set the Auto lift re-price flag. This will cause the Re-price flag to be automatically removed as soon as stock for it is received. If the product is a new release you would set the Auto lift flag to true. If the product is regularly brought in from overseas and re-priced each time as the exchange varies, set the flag to false.
(You can set the default setting of this flag in the Titles tab in System Options).

Order Entry window
When entering backorders for products flagged as "re-price", a red message will be displayed in the edit line Re-price on release.



Just save the line as you normally would and it's all done - the backorders will be flagged to be re-priced during backorder release.
However there are a few options:
·If you can partially supply a line (e.g. the order is for ten and you have three in stock), you will be given an option when saving the line, of whether you want the backorders re-priced or sent out at the existing price.  
·If you re-set the Sell price then the pricing and discounts used will be those you type in - in other words the re-pricing is cancelled.  
·If you leave the Sell and Price fields as they are and just amend the Discount field, then that discount will be taken off the re-priced amount. (For example, if you enter a discount of 10% and the product is later re-priced at $100, then the customer will be charged $90).  
·If you amend the Price field and leave the Sell and Discount fields as they are then, the product will be charged at that price, although the RRP will be later updated.  

Receiving window
When stock is received, the price will be set or updated in the title master.
If the Auto lift re-price flag has been set, the Re-price flag will be removed against the product.

Backorder Release window
During backorder release, the prices will be re-calculated to exactly the same values as if you were now entering the order in invoice entry.

Warning
Information on re-pricing is stored in the picking slip tables. Setting a titles re-price flag after the backorder has been created will not cause it to be re-priced. The flag must be set at the time the order is first entered.
