Order Entry

In the Order Entry window you can process customer orders, creating:
·invoices,  
·picking slips,  
·approvals (or inspection copies),  
·consignments outwards,  
·customer quotations,  
·pro forma invoices.  

Orders can be entered by:
·manually typing them in,  
·importing from your web site, (e-Web module)  
·importing from EDI invoice file, (wholesale)  
·appended from approvals, special orders, quotes and pro forma invoices.  

Also in the window, you can also process customer returns and other credits. These credits can be:
·manually entered,  
·appended from invoices (allowing you to easily reverse an invoice)  
·appended from returns authorisations. (wholesale)  
(Not all users have access to all these functions. It will depend on how your system is configured).

ORDER ENTRY WINDOW

The Order Entry window is opened from the main menu: Sales / Invoices / Invoice, Credit, Approval Entry.
Below is an outline of how to process customer orders

HEADER DETAILS

Step 1: Select the customer   
Select a customer in the Customer field in the usual way – enter the customer code in to the customer field, click the F3 button to open the customer search window or click the Alt F3 button to recall the last customer.
·The customer's name and default details will be displayed.  
·Details on their account balance are displayed - current, 30, 60 and 90 days.  
·For the distributor module, a summary of the customer's current orders and backorders is displayed.  
·For tax exempt customers, 'Tax Exempt' will be displayed in a red field.  
·For customers on stop, 'Stop' will be displayed in a red field and you will be unable to proceed further.  
·If the customer has exceeded their credit limit, 'Limit Exceeded' will be displayed in a red field. You will still be able to create an invoice.  
·Customers who are not account customers will will only be able to have Approvals and Quotations created for them unless the Mailorder flag is set in the Customer Master / Detail tab. When searching for a customer, account customers (debtors) are highlighted in aqua in the Customer drop down box.  

Step 2: Transaction header details
Now enter the header details for the transaction:
Enter the customer order number (optional). Order numbers can also be entered line by line.
You can change the:
·customer contact: the combo box lists all of that customer's contacts,  
·sales rep: the second sales rep field is for use where you have separate reps for different product types (e.g. books and music),  
·sales terms  
·delivery address: You can use the arrow buttons to select alternate delivery addresses or you can add a new address,  
·header discount off the transaction total. (You can optionally enter a discount off the total value of the transaction. This can also be entered (or changed) in the footer window. If a discount is entered here then the running total displayed as you are entering the order lines will reflect the discount. If the discount is dependent on the value of the invoice, you would enter it in the footer window).  
   
Step 3: Transaction type   
In the Transaction Type radio button group select the transaction type you require:
·Standard invoice is used to process customers' standard orders. It is used to produce an invoice, picking slip or forward order.  
·Credit note is used for processing customer returns and correcting invoice errors.  
·Approval is for sending out goods on approval to customers.  
·Consignment outwards is available only with the Consignment module. It is used for sending goods on consignment to retailer customers.  
·Pro forma invoice will create a pro forma invoice. It will not reduce your stock levels.  
·Quotation will create a customer quotation. It will not reduce your stock levels.  

Step 4: Transaction mode   
In the Transaction mode radio buttons group select the required option:

Invoices, approvals and consignment orders
 
Retail configuration
·Stock adjusting: the transaction will reduce stock and create a standard invoice, approval advice or consignment advice.  
·Charge only: (invoices only) the transaction will not reduce your stock levels but will create an invoice. It can be used to correct undercharges on an invoice.  

Credit notes
·   Return to stock: the transaction reduce stock and create a standard invoice.  

Distributor/publisher configuration
·Picking slip will produce a picking slip for the warehouse (or back office) to process. The transaction will reduce your stock.  
·Already sent will create a standard invoice, approval advice or consignment advice and reduce the stock level.  
·Charge only: (invoices only) the invoice will not reduce your stock levels but will create an invoice. It can be used to correct undercharges on an invoice.  
·Forward order will create a picking slip to be processed at a future date. It will reduce your stock levels.  

Credit notes
·Return to stock: the transaction reduce stock and create a standard invoice.  
·No stock movement: the transaction will not reduce your stock levels. This option can be used to correct overcharges on an invoice or for crediting goods lost in transit.  

Step 5: Saving/displaying the header
When you have finished with the header, click on the Save button (F12).
At any time you can return to the header by clicking on the Show Header button (F11).

Important option
You can quickly and easily batch invoice all your special orders here. See the Batch processing section of the Special Order chapter for details.

ENTERING THE ORDER

Step 6: Processing a line
·In the ISBN field: scan the barcode or type in the ISBN. Alternatively you can use either one the various shortcut title searches, the F2 title search button or the Alt F2 button to recall the last barcode used.  
·The Order No field: for the first line this will display the order number entered in the header. If you enter a new order number, subsequent lines will default to this new number.  
·In the Order Qty box, enter the quantity ordered.  

Retail configuration
·In the Supplied box, enter the quantity you can supply.  
·Where their insufficient stock to fill the order line, you will be given the option to put the item on special order when you save the line to the browser. See below in the 'Backorder' section of 'Options'.  

Distributor/publisher configuration
·In the Supply box, the quantity you can supply will be displayed.  
·   Any backordered quantity will be displayed in the B/Order field.  
·You can apply a discount against a single line here. (To apply the same discount for the whole sale, do it in step 11).  
·You can also add a Comment against the title here.  
·The running total of the transaction will display in the Order value field.  
·Click Save (F12) to save the line to the browser. Alternatively click Clear Line (Esc) to delete it.  

Step 7: Displaying the footer window
Repeat step 7 for each line of the invoice. Click Post (F12) to open the footer window.

OPTIONS
Editing
To edit any line you have saved, double click on the line in the browser. The line will be copied into the input line in the header.
Overtype any changes you want to make and click on the Save button.
To delete a saved line, right click on it in the browser and select Delete from the drop down menu.

Free text
To add a message line to an order, type an asterisk ( * ) into the barcode field. The free text line will appear. Type in your message and then click on the Save button (F12).

Backorders
Backordering is enabled by checking the Auto Backorder checkbox in the Copies tab of System Options.
When this flag is set, a special order (backorder) will be automatically created during invoice posting if the Supplied quantity is less than the Order quantity. There is a option F7 to display the Status of the title on such lines – to give advice on the printed invoice copy and update the title master status. If you wish to use this Status option you should ensure the Status "Backorder" column is ticked on all status that you wish to backorder on (Tables/Authority Tables/Status window by editing the status and ticking "all backordering' or alternative putting cursor over the backorder field in the list against each title and double clicking).


Faster input
For faster input where you are processing single quantities with no discounts or line comments and with the same order number, set the Singles (F6) checkbox to true. Then simply scan or type in the barcode of each product and it will be saved automatically to the browser.

Appending
When invoicing, you can append approvals, special orders, pro forma invoices and quotations.When entering a credit note, you can append an invoice to reverse it.
Wholesale (distributor/publisher configuration only).
Note that to append an approval you must select invoice type 'Charge Only', as the stock will already have been removed from your inventory.
When entering a credit note, you can append an invoice to reverse it or from a returns authorization.


How to append a transaction
·Click on the Append button.  
·The Append From combo box will be displayed. This will display all the transaction numbers for this customer that are available to be appended into this transaction:  
·special orders where stock has been received,  
·quotations,  
·pro forma invoices,  
·approvals ( if 'Charge Only' has been selected)  
·invoices,  
·return authorisations.  



·Select the transaction you wish to append (e.g. Quote 367425 )  
·A pop up box will be displayed showing all the lines that can be appended.  
·To de-select a line double click on the Use column.  
·To reduce the quantity to be appended, double click in the Quantity column and overtype it. Note that for approvals, you will not be able to edit the quantity.  



·Click the OK button or press the Enter key and the selected lines will be copied into the browser. You can then edit any line by double clicking on it to copy it into the edit line.  
·Click Save (F12) to save your changes.  

Appending another customer's transactions
The Append From combo box display displays only the approvals, specials etc for that customer. To append transactions from another customer, right click on the window to open the context menu and select one of the five Append options.

Results of appending
·Special orders: when the invoice is posted, the system will update the special orders file to show that special order as invoiced.  
·Approvals: when the invoice is posted, the system will update the approval file to show that approval as invoiced.  
·Invoices: appending an invoice to a credit note. On posting, the program will automatically allocate the credit against the invoice in the debtors file. You can turn off this feature for individual customers by setting the 'no auto allocate' flag on Financial tab of the customer master.  

Backorders, stock availabilityand due dates
Distributor/publisher configuration only
You can set a stock availability message against each status type in the Status Maintenance window (main menu: Tables/Authority tables/ Status).
The message will print as the backorder reason against each backorder line of your invoice. For example you may set 'temporarily out of stock' against the 'active' status.

In the Order entry window, this message displays in red on the edit line.


You can edit this field and set a due date for the delivery by clicking the F7 button. This opens the Availability pop-up window. Set the due date and a new availability message and click the OK button.




ENTERING THE FOOTER DETAILS

Step 8: Footer details
·Apply a discount off the sale total.  
·Add a freight charge.  
·Add a surcharge. (If wholesale)  
·You can add up to three lines of header Comments.  

Step 9: Applying payments
To apply a payment click on the Payment button (F9). This will open the Tendering window.



·The default transaction type will be that set on the Financial tab of System Options.  
·The Purchases, Total Sale, Owing and Amount Tendered fields will display the sale total including backorders.  
·If there are backorders, the total excluding backorders is displayed just above the Amount Tendered field. To accept payment for just the lines in stock, click on the Use button. (Use button will act as a toggle between including and excluding backorders). In the retail configuration, the backorder will take the form of a special order. Any payment for it will be stored as a deposit on the special order.  
·The Tendering window process is the same as that in the POS window. Refer to that section for details.  
·Note: for non-debtor customers the invoice must be paid in full to process. You will know that this is required by the Required message, as shown in the image below:  



Step 10: Print options
·Print Preview: choose this option when you are processing single quantities of goods with no discounts or line comments and with the same order number. Simply scan the barcode of each product.  
·Batch print: when the box is unchecked invoices are printed as they are posted. When ticked, invoices will be printed when you close the window at the end of the session. You can also start the batch printing by opening the context menu (right mouse click anywhere in the window) and selecting 'print batched transactions'.  
·Carton labels: if you wish to print carton labels, tick Print checkbox. For more than one label, overtype the label quantity in the 'No:' field. You can print more carton labels after you have posted by opening the context menu (right mouse click anywhere in the window) and selecting 'reprint invoice labels'.  

Step 11: Posting
·Click the Post button (F12) to post the transaction.  
·If the customer is not a debtor and full payment has not been entered, the Tendering window open allowing you to record the payment (See Step 10 above.)  
·When the invoice has posted a pop up window will display with the invoice number.  
·For users of marketing module upon posting it will display the marketing window. See the Point of Sale chapter for details of the Marketing window. Note: the marketing window will only display for invoices and credit notes.
