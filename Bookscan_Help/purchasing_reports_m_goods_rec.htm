Purchasing Report:      Goods Received History


   Purpose:
Report goods received (with variance between GRN and Invoice amounts) in a date-range.  

   Parameters:
Date-range.  

   Output:
Date received, supplier, GRN amount, Invoiced amount, Variance.  
Subtotals by supplier.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
Report Title       -   expresses the date-range spanned by the report.  

   

See also:   Selection Controls.
