Sales Report:   Overdue Special Orders


   Purpose:
Report special orders yet to be filled.  

   Parameters:
Start and Finish dates, Supplier(s), option to include, exclude or report-only standing orders.  

   Output:
Title, ISBN, Qty & Date ordered, Special & Master comments, Customer (& suburb), Status.  
Grouped by Supplier.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle   -   Reflects the selection-parameters.
