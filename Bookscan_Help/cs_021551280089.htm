Printing Approval Reports
Previous  Top  Next


To reprint an approval:

Step 1:
From the main menu, click on Sales, then point to Approvals and then Print. Click on Reprint.

Step 2:   
Click on the down arrow and scroll down and select the approval number from the list.

Step 3:
Click OK to reprint.


Other reports:

All   
Prints a report of all outstanding approvals.
Option: Include approvals that have been filled.

Summary
Prints a summary of outstanding approvals.
Options: Sort by customer or by date.

14 Days
Prints a summary of approvals over 14 days old.
Options: Change aging date.
Include approvals that have been filled.  

Customer   
Prints a summary report of approvals for selected customers.
Hold the control button down and click on the customers in the list that you wish to include in the report.
