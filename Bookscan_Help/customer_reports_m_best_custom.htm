Customer Report:       Best Customers


   Purpose:
Comparative listing of best customers in quantitative terms.  
        
 
   Parameters:
Any number of categories may be included unless the "All Customers" radio-button is pressed.  
Cutoff value (to limit extent of "bestness" reported).  
Start and finish dates.  
 
   Output:
Customer name, Customer key, Total Sales, Discount Amount, Quantity, YTD Sales, Profit.  
 

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.
