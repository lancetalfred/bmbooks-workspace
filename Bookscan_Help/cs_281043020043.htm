Purchase orders by suppliers
You can see a list of the purchase orders for this supplier by clicking the Supp's POs button.

Purchase orders by ISBN
Alternatively, to narrow the search, you can see a list of purchase orders by ISBN:
· Click in the Barcode/ISBN text box,  
· Scan the barcode (or type in the ISBN).  
· Click the PO on Code button.  
· A list all purchase orders containing that item will be displayed.  
· To select a purchase order, double click on it.  
· To close the list, click on the Hide XXXX button.
