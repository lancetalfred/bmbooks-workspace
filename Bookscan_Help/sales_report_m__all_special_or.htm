Sales Report:   All Special Orders


   Purpose:
Report all special orders on file (with an optional starting-number).  

   Parameters:
Starting (order) number, option to include/exclude filled orders, sort-option.  

   Output:
Title, Comments, Order-date, Qty ordered, received & delivered, Status, OrderNo; Master comments.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle   -   Reflects the selection criteria.  
    lWithSubTotals   -   Determines if Customer subtotals will be printed.

See also:   Selection Controls
