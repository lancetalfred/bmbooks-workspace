Ordering
To create a purchase order for consignment stock, click on the consignment check box (or press F4) for each consignment line.
The purchase order for consignment lines will show This order line for consignment stock.



Receiving
When receiving stock, those lines that were ordered as consignments will have the Consignment box checked when you scan them in. You can turn any line into consignment stock by just clicking on the check box . Otherwise consignment receipts are processed in the same way as ordinary receipts.
When consignment receipts are posted, the On Hand and the Consignment figures in the title master are increased.

Sales
Bookscan automatically keeps track of your consignment sales. If stock of a product is a mix of consignment and purchased stock, the system always assumes that you sell your purchased stock first. When that is all sold, sales are recorded as consignment sales.

Reconciliation
Periodically (usually monthly), you will need to advise your suppliers of your consignment sales. You do this in the Consignment Reconciliation window.
From the main menu select Sales / Consignments In to open this window.



Step 1 Update sales
In the Update to field select the date up to which you wish to process. Then click on the Update sales button.
The system will search the sales history and update the consignment sales quantity (Sales) and average sales amount (Sales $) columns for each product in the browser. In the above example, there were 39 sales of "Rainmaker" at an average price of $17.64. The cost price, recorded during receiving, was $8.82. The Extended cost price was $343.98.

Step 2 Make amendments, shrinkage, correct returns, buy the stock
Double clicking on a line in the browser will copy it into the edit line. Here you can:
·Amend the cost price if required.  
·If the cost price is to be calculated as a percentage of the average sell price, type that percentage into the Discount field and press the Tab key and the cost price will be recalculated.  
·If you are have decided to pay for the stock before selling it, enter the quantity to be sold in the Buy field.  
·If there has been any stock shrinkage and you are now going to pay for these items, enter the quantity in the Shrinkage field.  
·If you have returned the stock to the supplier, but have not processed them as consignment returns, enter the quantity returned in the Returned field. This will reduce the quantity on consignment but leave the on hand figure untouched.  
Note: This procedure is only used to make corrections where stock was returned as non-consignment stock. If you want to process consignment returns, do so in the Draft returns window (main menu - Purchasing / Returns / Add / change )  
·Click on Save line (F12) to save the changes.  

Step 3 Select invoice or sales advice option
In the Print area, select either Sales Advice to produce a Consignment Sales Advice report or Invoice to create an invoice and update your creditors files.

Step 4 Process
Clicking the Process button produces your Consignment Invoice / Sales Advice reports. It will also update the consignment browser and the consignment field title master.

Right Mouse Options
Right click on the Consignment Reconciliation window to display these menu options:

Title master
Display the title master for the current product selected in the browser.

Delete consignment
Delete the currently selected consignment line.

Mark as complete
For the currently selected consignment line, sets the outstanding quantity as returned.

Show processing history
Opens a window showing a summary the consignment sale advices for the currently selected consignment line.
You can select a consignment advice here and reprint it.

Reprint consignment advice
Opens a window where you can select a consignment advice and reprint it.

Print summary
Prints a hard copy of the current browser. If the display is filtered by supplier, it will print a consignment listing for just that supplier. If no filter is set, the report lists consignments for all suppliers.
