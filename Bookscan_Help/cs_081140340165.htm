Promotions
Previous  Top  Next

 
You can use the Promotions module to change temporarily both the selling price and the cost price of promotions stock.  
 
When selling promotion stock within the specified dates, the selling price in the Cash Sales, Layby and Invoice screens will default to the promotion price.  
 
Similarly, if the supplier is offering a special price, enter the price and the promotion dates in the Promotions screen.  
Then, when receiving stock within those dates, the cost price in the Receiving screen will default to the special price.  
 
Opening the Promotions window  
In the main menu, click on Table, then point to Pricing and then click on Promotions.  
 


The Maintain Promotions window will open.  
This comprises two browsers - the upper one lists all the current promotions and the lower one lists the items on the promotion highlighted in the upper browser.
To show expired promotions click on the Show Expired Promotions button.  
 
Add a new promotion  
· Click on the Add Promo (F12) button and fill in the promotion details:  
 
Code   Unique six digit key for the promotion  
   (e.g. XMAS04).  
 
Name    Full name of the promotion.  
 
Start   Start date for the promotion.  
 
End   Last day of the promotion.  
 
Orders start   Start date for supplier's special prices.  
 
Order End   End date for supplier's special prices.     
 
· When finished click the Save Promo (F12) button.  
 
Edit a promotion
·Double click on the promotion in the upper browser.  
·The various promotion fields will filled.  
·Edit these as required.  
·Click the Save Promo (F12) button to save your changes or Clear Promo (Esc) button to discard them.  

Delete a new promotion
·Right click on the promotion in the upper browser to open the context menu.  
·Select Delete promotion.  

Adding titles to the promotion  
·Highlight the required promotion in the upper browser.  
·Enter the product code or scan the barcode in the ISBN field.  
·Enter the Promotion Sell Price.  
·Enter the Promotion Cost Price.  
·Click Save Line (F12) when finished.  

Edit a promotion line
·Double click on the line in the lower browser.  
·The various promotion lines fields will filled.  
·Edit these as required.  
·Click the Save Line (F12) button to save your changes or Clear Line (Esc) button to discard them.  

Delete a new promotion
·Right click on the promotion line in the lower browser to open the context menu.  
·Select Delete line from promotion.
