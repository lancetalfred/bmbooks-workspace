Sales Report:   Title-Quantities Sold Monthly


   Purpose:
Summarise sale-quantities by month (by department or supplier).  

   Parameters:
Sort-order, Cutoff quantity, Month and (if not sorted by quantity) nominated suppliers or departments.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Author, On-hand, Last Sale-date, 12 columns of monthly sales quantities plus one totalling column.  
Subtotalled either by supplier or by department.  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  
SortOrder      -   determines sort-order.  
Cutoff      -   the lowest level of sales to include in the report.  

See also:   Selection Controls.
