From the main menu, select Purchasing, then Transfers, then Reports and then click on the report you require.

Reprints
Use this option to reprint individual transfers.
Enter the transfer number by either typing it in the Transfer Number box or click on the down arrow and select the number from the list.

Transfers out   
This lists a summary of all outbound transfers out within the dates you specify,
Select the start and finished dates you require and click OK.

Transfers in
This lists a summary of all inwards transfers within the dates you specify,
Select the start and finished dates you require and click OK.
