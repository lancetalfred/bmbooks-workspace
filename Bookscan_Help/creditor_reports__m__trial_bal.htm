Creditor Report:         Trial Balance


   Purpose:
    Review current, 30-day, 60-day and 90-day creditor balances.

   Parameters:
Currencies to include, option to include Zero-balance creditors, Local suppliers only, Summary output option.  

   Output:
Creditor detail (unless in summary, which reports only currency totals), periodic balances, YTD.  

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle      -   Reflects selection criteria.
MultiCurrency   -   Controls printing of report's third section.
    Summary      -   Controls format of report's first section.
    SuppTot      -   Total balance for third section of report.
    Op_Bal      -   Opening balance (for third section of report).

See also:   Selection Controls.
