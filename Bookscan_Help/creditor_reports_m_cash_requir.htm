Creditor Report:         Cash Requirement


   Purpose:
    Review creditors' cash requirements with regard to nominated date and currencies.

   Parameters:
    Due date, Currencies to be included.

   Output:
    Requirements in 30-day increments, by creditor and in currency-summary.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle      -   Reflects selection criteria.
    HomeCurrency   -   The code for the local currency.
    ShowHome      -   Controls whether the report prints currency conversions.
    cRatesUsed   -   Informs report's second section of conversion rates.
   
See also:   Selection Controls.
