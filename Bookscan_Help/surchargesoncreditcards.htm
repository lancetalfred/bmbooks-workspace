Surcharges on credit cards
Previous  Top  Next

Many users want to apply a surcharge on certain types of credit cards which charge higher fees. In the transaction window set percentage charges against the relevant credit card types, say 1.5 % on Diners Club and 2.5% on Amex.
When you select these transactions in the Tendering window, the surcharge is added to the total price. You can overwrite the percentage in the Tendering window if you wish.
