Archiving Old Titles
Previous  Top  Next


Archiving titles removes them from the title master and stores them in the archive file. Earlier versions of Bookscan only saved selected fields from the title master to the archive. In the present version of the program all fields are retained.


All sales history is retained.

Why archive
By keeping the size of the title master down, you speed up searches. Archived title can be easily restored to the title master. In the Title Master, select New (F11) once you enter the ISBN in the Code field and tab or enter, Bookscan will scan the archive to see if there is an archived record and ask if you want to recall it, if one is found.



Archiving
·First re-index your master file and do your daily backup.  
·Open the Archiving window from the main menu: Utilities\Archives.  
·Select the date the titles were last sold.  
·Select the date the titles were last purchased  
·Select the date the titles were created.  
·Only titles with no stock, no special orders and none on order will be archived.
