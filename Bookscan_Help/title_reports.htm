Title Reports


   Select a report to view details of selection-options, purpose, output and variables:

   Supplier          -   Reports all titles for (nominated) supplier(s).
   Author            -   All titles by author.
   Title            -   All titles in alphabetical order.
   Category         -   All titles from nominated categories.
   Department         -   All titles in (nominated) department(s)   
   Imprint            -   Report titles by imprints.
   Status            -   Report titles by status.
   Hotlist            -   Performance of hotlist titles.
   On Hold         -   All or nominated titles on hold.
   On Order         -   Customers with nominated title on order.
   Buyers            -   Purchasers of a specific title.
   Sales            -   Reports all sales.
   Valuation
      All         -   Values all on-hand stock
      Departments (all)   -   Values all stock by department
      Department (single)   -   Values stock for selected department
      Supplier      -   Values nominated supplier's stock
      Errors         -   Reports values outside nominated ranges
      Aged Stock      -   Identify (and/or mark down prices on) non-selling stock.
