Purchasing Report:      Credit Claim


   Purpose:
Reprint credit claims.  

   Parameters:
Credit claim number.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Qty Returned, Invoice number, Invoice date, Invoice costs & RRPs.  
Totals within claim (including freight).  
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
Licensee's address    -   a number of variables contribute to this.  
ClaimNo         -    the credit claim number.  
    SuppAccount      -    the supplier's account number.   

See also:   Selection Controls.
