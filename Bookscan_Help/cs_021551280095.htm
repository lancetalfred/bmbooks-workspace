Deleting Special Orders
Previous  Top  Next

 
Step 1:  
From the main menu, click on Sales, then point to Specials and click on Enquire.  
The Special Order Enquiry window will now open.  
 
Step 2:  
In the Special Order field, type in the order number or click on the down arrow and select it from the drop down list.
All the lines on the special order will be displayed in the browser.
If you don't know the special order number, you can find it using the three search fields. See Special Order Enquiries.

Step 3:   
To delete a line from the special order:
· Highlight the line by clicking on it.  
· Click the Delete Line button.  
· Click on Yes to confirm the deletion.  
        
To delete the whole order:  
· Click the Delete order button.  
· Click on Yes to confirm the deletion.  
 
If there is a deposit outstanding on the special order, you will be given the opportunity to refund it. If you do not wish to, ie customer defaulted, you can set the refund to zreo by overtyping the amount displayed
