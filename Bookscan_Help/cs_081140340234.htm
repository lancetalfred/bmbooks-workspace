At the End of Day Sales Close notification will show , reporting that the Nielsen Bookscan FILES ARE BEING PROCESSED.
This is confirmation that the Nielsen Bookscan file are ready to be sent.

Sending to Nielsen Bookscan
To send the data follow the following steps.
Step 1
Open from the Bookscan main menu by clicking e-Comms/e-Comms.

Step 2
Click File, then click Transfer. Then click Start button.

Step 3
e-Comms will automatically send data via FTP.
This will only send if there is data available to be sent.
