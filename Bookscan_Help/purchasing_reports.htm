Purchasing Reports

   Select a report below, to view details of selection-options, purpose, output and variables:


   Supplier Draft POs         -   Reports all Draft POs for nominated supplier(s).
   Final
      PO Number         -   Print nominated purchase order.
      All            -   Reports all purchase orders.
      Supplier         -   Outstanding POs with nominated supplier.
      Back Orders         -   All back orders.
      Back Orders per Supplier   -   All back orders with nominated supplier.
      PO Slips         -   Used by Bookscan (Report Writer can modify it).
      Promotions (promotions module must be enabled)
         PO Number      -   Print nominated promotional purchase order.
         All         -   Reports all promotional purchase orders.
         Supplier      -   Outstanding promo POs with nominated supplier.
         Back Orders      -   All promotional back orders.
         B-Orders per Supplier   -   All promotional back orders with nominated supplier.
         
   Returns
      Draft            -   Print (nominated) draft credit claims.
      Credit Claim         -   Reprint a credit claim.
      All Credit Claims      -   All within a specified date-range.   
      Reprint Returns Authorisation   -   Wholesalers' RA report.
   Goods Received History      -   Goods received history between chosen dates.
   Reprint GRN(s)            -   Print GRNs for unposted goods.
   Transfers
      Reprint             -   Reprint nominated transfer-number.
      Out            -   List outbound transfers between chosen dates.
      In            -   List inbound transfers between chosen dates.
