Customer Reports

   Select a report below, to view details of selection-options, purpose, output and variables:


   Individual      -   Reports purchases for a single customer.
   All         -   Reports purchases for all customers.
   Best Customers    -   Comparative listing of customers (by quantity of purchases).
   Most Profitable    -   Comparative listing of customers (by profitability of purchases).
   Quantity / Cost    -   Reports the quantity/cost of selected purchases.
   Mail Out      _   Create a customer listing, mailing labels or a merge file.
