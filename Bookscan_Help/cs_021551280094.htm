Special orders where the product in not known

Previous  Top  Next


You can create a draft special order for a title whose ISBN you have not yet been able to identify. This allows you to create a special order at the POS and then later, when you have more time, look up the ISBN.

Creating the draft special order
Open the special order create window and enter your customer in the usual way.
Click on the Unknown (F8) button (or enter an asterisk in the barcode field and then press the Tab or Enter key). A pop up window will be displayed where you can enter an approximation of the title and the author. The title field is a required field.



The program will insert an asterisk for the barcode and set the supplier to the miscellaneous supplier (code 'MISC'). You can change this to the actual supplier if you know it. To change it back to MISC, click the Unknown (F9) button.
You can mix known and unknown titles on a special order.
To reopen the Title/Author pop up window of a line, double click on the line in the browser.
After entering all the lines of the special order, save it in the usual way.

Processing draft special orders.
·Open the draft special order window from the main menu: Sales/Specials/Replace.  
·The window will display all outstanding draft special orders in the browser.  
·Double click on the line you wish to process.  
·The line will be copied onto the Suspected Title and Suspected Author fields and the cursor will move to the Enter Replacement ISBN field.  
·Type in the replacement ISBN or click the F2 button to open the title master to search for the title. (If you have just added the ISBN to the database, or just looked it up, you can instead just click on the Recall button).  



·Once you have found the title, click the Replace button. It now becomes a normal special order.  
·If you already have stock of the new title, the Allocate from Stock window will open allowing you to allocate stock against this order.
