Quick Input

Check the Quick Input box when:
Retail module  
·you are not discounting any line.  
·the quantity is always one.  
·all lines have the same order number  
·you are not entering line comments.  
Then just scan each title .
Click on the Post button when done.

Wholesale module  
·you are not discounting any line.  
·all lines have the same order number.  
·you are not entering line comments.  
Then just enter each title and the quantity and press the Enter key.
Click on the Post button when done.
