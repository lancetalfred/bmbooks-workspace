Purchasing Report:      Transfers Out


   Purpose:
List outgoing transfers within a nominated date-range.  

   Parameters:
Start and finish dates.  

   Output:
ISBN, Title, Author, Date, Quantity transferred, To location, From location, Costing.  

 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
nReportClass       -   The type of report being run.  
    dStart and dFinish      -   The date-range values.
   

See also:   Selection Controls.
