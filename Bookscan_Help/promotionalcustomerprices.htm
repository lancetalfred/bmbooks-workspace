Promotional Customer Prices

If you have special prices for customers for certain titles, you can enter the details here. When entering invoices,laybys and cash  
sales for this customer, the system will automatically select the special price.  
 
Opening the Promotions window  
In the main menu, click on Tables/Pricing/Promotional Customer Prices.  
 
The Special Customer Prices window will open.  
 
Title discounted for several customers  
In the Prices on ISBN window, scan the barcode or type in the ISBN.  
All the customers with special prices for this product will be displayed ion the left hand browser.
To add a customer, click the left hand Add button.  
Select the customer and enter the customer's special price.  
Click OK .

Listing by customer  
In the Customer field select the customer.  
All the products for which this customers has special prices will be displayed ion the right hand browser.
To add a product, click the right hand Add button.  
Select the product and enter the customer's special price.  
Click OK .
