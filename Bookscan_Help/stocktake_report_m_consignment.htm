Stocktake Report:      Consignment


   Purpose:
    Report stocktake consignment quantities.

   Parameters:
    Coverage by (nominated) department, departmental group or all.

   Output:
    Title, Author, Costings and Consignment quantity (subtotalled by Department) .

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    ReportTitle   -   Denotes the scope of the stocktake being reported.
    lSubtotals    -   Determines whether or not data is subtotalled.

   
See also:   Selection Controls.
