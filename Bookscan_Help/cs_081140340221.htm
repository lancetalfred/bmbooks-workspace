The Pre-Pack Module
Previous  Top  Next


The pre-pack module assists booksellers in partnering schools to deliver required books (and other items such as stationery and school levies - collectively known as booklists) to the students.

Select Modules\Pre-pack, from Bookscan's main menu, and you will open a submenu of six items. They represent the major components of developing, selling, ordering and being paid for book-lists. They will be disabled if the module is not installed. To install Pre-pack, select System Options from the Utilities menu. (The option is grayed out if any other Bookscan windows are opened.) Click the tab with the caption Modules and check the Pre-pack control. Checking it will enable some other controls which help to tailor the way that Pre-pack operates.

The pre-pack submenu gives you access to these components:

   A:   Developing book-lists.
   B:   Logging the students' requests.
   C:   Packing and invoicing
   D:   Ordering
   E:   Pre-pack Reports
   F:    Year Rollover
