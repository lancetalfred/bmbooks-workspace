Valuation Report:      Departments (all)


   Purpose:
Value all stock on-hand and report by department and/or consolidated department.  

   Parameters:
    Omit consolidation option. Department codes are consolidated in sharing their first character.

   Output:
    Dept, No. of Titles & Items, Value at: Last & Average costs, Sell & R.r.p; Departmental percentage of stock.

   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
    None.
