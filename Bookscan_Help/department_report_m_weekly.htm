Department Report:       Weekly


   Purpose:
Compare up to nineteen weeks of sales by department (across current and prior year).  
 
   Parameters:
Starting week.  
 
   Output:
Department name, 19 columns - each reflecting one week of sales.  
 
 
   Report-form Variables reset at runtime:
ReportTitle    -   reflects the selection parameters.  
StartWeek   -   determines how columns are labelled.  
cLastYear   -   first year of the comparison.  
cThisYear   -   second year of the comparison.  
lByDept   -   indicates whether the report is being run by Department. It can also be run "by Supplier".  


See also:   Selection Controls.
